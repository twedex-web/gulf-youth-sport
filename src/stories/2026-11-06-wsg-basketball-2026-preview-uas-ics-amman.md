The World School Games are back in Valencia, and the Middle East is on court. Schools from across the region and Europe have arrived at L'Alqueria del Basket for the 2026 WSG Basketball Championships, with the action this weekend, Saturday 7 and Sunday 8 November. Boys' and girls' teams compete at U16 and U19.

Two Middle East schools stand out as ones to watch.

## UAS: Dubai's Young Guns

Universal American School bring an U16 boys' squad to Valencia. For a young group, it is a big stage: a weekend of games against school teams from across Europe, at one of Spain's best-known basketball venues, home to Valencia Basket's academy. It is a chance to test themselves against new styles of play, and a valuable early-season run-out for the Dubai side.

## ICS Amman: The Former Champions

ICS Amman know exactly what it takes to win here. The Jordanian school are former WSG Basketball champions, and their U19 boys return to Valencia with a reputation to defend and every intention of adding to their record.
