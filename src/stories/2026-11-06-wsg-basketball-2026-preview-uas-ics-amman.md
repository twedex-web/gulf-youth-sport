Two Middle East schools are in Spain this weekend for the 2026 WSG Basketball Championships. Dubai's Universal American School (UAS) and Jordan's ICS Amman join 14 other schools from across Europe at L'Alqueria del Basket in Valencia, with the action on court on Saturday 7 and Sunday 8 November.

Teams compete in U16 and U19 age groups, for boys and girls.

## UAS Bring Dubai to Valencia

The UAE's only representatives, UAS, take an U16 boys' squad to Valencia. It is a big stage for a young group: a weekend of games against school teams from across Europe, at one of Spain's best-known basketball venues, home to Valencia Basket's academy.

## ICS Amman Return as Former Champions

ICS Amman know what it takes to win here. The Jordanian school are former WSG Basketball champions, and bring their U19 boys back to Valencia looking to add to that record.

## The Opposition

Ireland sends the biggest contingent, with six schools: Castleknock Community College, Presentation Secondary School, Carrigtwohill Community College, Mungret Community College (with both a boys' and a girls' team), St Mary's CBS and Coláiste Éanna.

The rest of the field comes from across the continent: ACS Cobham from England, the International School of Geneva, Cambridge School Bucharest, Agora Andorra International School, Poland's School Complex in Szubin and Akademeia High School Warsaw, and Spanish sides Barcelona High School and IES Jaume el Just II.
