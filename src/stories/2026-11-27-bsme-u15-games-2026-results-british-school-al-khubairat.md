Nineteen schools from the UAE and Kuwait came to Deira International School last weekend for the BSME U15 Games, three days of athletics, football, volleyball and netball. When the points from all six competitions were added up, British School Al Khubairat stood on top.

## Two Titles and Nothing Lower Than Fifth

BSAK's title came from winning when it mattered and never having a bad day. Their netballers took gold ahead of Cranleigh Abu Dhabi and Dubai British School Jumeirah Park, and their boys won the football, with The British International School Abu Dhabi runners-up and South View School taking bronze. Add silver in both the girls' and boys' athletics, bronze in the boys' volleyball and fifth in the girls' football, and no other school came close to that consistency. In the boys' athletics, BSAK's Ben was named Athlete of the Day.

## Brighton College Abu Dhabi Strike Gold Twice

Brighton College Abu Dhabi finished second overall on the back of two golds. They won the boys' athletics, ahead of BSAK and Royal Grammar School Guildford, and the boys' volleyball, where Marko was named Player of the Tournament. The British International School Abu Dhabi took volleyball silver, with BSAK in third. Brighton's girls added a football bronze.

## A Big Day for BISAD's Girls

The British International School Abu Dhabi completed the overall podium. Their girls won the athletics, ahead of BSAK and North London Collegiate School, and Madeline was named Athlete of the Day. Their silver medals in both the boys' football and the boys' volleyball made sure the points came from across the school.

## Football Gold for North London Collegiate

The girls' football belonged to North London Collegiate School, who beat Sunmarke School to the title, with Brighton College Abu Dhabi in third. Sunmarke's Esme was named Player of the Tournament. Add bronze in the girls' athletics and fourth in the boys', and North London Collegiate climbed to sixth overall.

Some of the best individual performances came from schools further down the table. Emily of Dubai British School Jumeirah Park was the netball Player of the Tournament, helping her school to bronze and fifth place overall, and Selim of Royal Grammar School Guildford was named Player of the Tournament in the boys' football. The hosts had their best result in the boys' football, finishing fourth, while Gulf British Academy, one of the two Kuwaiti schools, reached eighth in the volleyball.

Behind the top three overall, Cranleigh Abu Dhabi finished fourth, followed by Dubai British School Jumeirah Park, North London Collegiate School, Royal Grammar School Guildford, Brighton College Dubai, Nord Anglia International School Abu Dhabi and Jumeirah College. Full standings for every sport are on the [BSME website](https://www.bsme.org.uk/bsme-u15-games-2026-the-results).

Under BSME's updated safeguarding policy, photos and videos from the Games are not being posted publicly. The galleries are password protected and shared directly with the schools that took part: families should check with their school for access.
