The Middle East's two flag-bearers at the WSG Basketball Championships came home from Valencia with very different stories to tell. ICS Amman's U19 boys went deep into the weekend, reaching the last four of the Cup. For Universal American School's U16s, it was a steep learning curve against some of the strongest young teams in Valencia.

## ICS Amman Live Up to Their Billing

The former champions started fast. ICS Amman opened on Saturday morning with a 31-25 win over England's ACS Cobham, then beat the International School of Geneva 27-20 and Barcelona High School 31-21. Their only group defeat came against Ireland's Mungret Community College, 41-30, which left ICS Amman second in Group A and through to the Cup semi-finals.

There they met the tournament's outstanding team. Agora Andorra International School won 61-19 on their way to the title, beating Mungret 54-36 in the final. ICS Amman then lost the bronze-medal game 41-30 to Ireland's Coláiste Éanna and finished fourth in the U19 boys' Cup.

Three wins and a top-four finish in a ten-team field is a solid weekend's work for the Jordanian school.

## A Tough Introduction for UAS

Universal American School knew Valencia would test them, and so it proved. The Dubai side were drawn in an U16 group that included three Valencia sides: Claret Benimaclet, Moncada and Valencia Basket Azul, the academy team of the city's professional club. Those sides finished first, second and third, and Claret Benimaclet went on to win the Cup, beating Moncada 55-39 in the final.

UAS did not win a game, but their closest contest showed what they can do. Against Poland's Akademeia High School Warsaw they went down by just three points, 21-18. The two teams met again in the Plate on Sunday, when UAS scored 20 points, their best total of the weekend, before losing 32-20 to finish sixth.

For a young squad, two days against that level of opposition are an experience to build on, and the UAS players now know exactly the standard it takes to compete in Valencia.
