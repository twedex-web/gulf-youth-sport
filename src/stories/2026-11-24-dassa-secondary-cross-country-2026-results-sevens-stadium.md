It was one of the biggest days of the DASSA calendar so far. On Sunday 22 November, The Sevens Stadium hosted the DASSA Secondary Cross Country Team Sprint Championships, with more than 1,100 athletes in 240 teams from 48 schools taking part across 12 races.

Schools raced in two series, Competitive and Development, with U13, U15 and Senior races for boys and girls in each.

## Competitive Series: JESS Arabian Ranches on Top

JESS Arabian Ranches A were crowned overall Competitive Series winners, ahead of Dubai College A, with JESS Arabian Ranches B completing the podium in third.

The A team's consistency did the damage: they finished in the top three in five of the six races, winning the Senior Girls and taking second in the U13 Boys, U13 Girls, U15 Boys and Senior Boys.

Dubai College A were just as busy on the podium, winning both the U15 Boys and Senior Boys races, while Arcadia British School won both the U13 Girls and U15 Girls. Safa Community School A took the U13 Boys title.

## Development Series: Ambassador International Academy Lead the Way

Ambassador International Academy won the Development Series overall, ahead of Fairgreen International School in second and Royal Grammar School Guildford B in third.

Ambassador won the U15 Girls race and finished second in both Senior races. Fairgreen won the U13 Boys and took second in the U15 Boys, and Repton School Dubai won both the U13 Girls and Senior Boys. Universal American School took the U15 Boys and GEMS International School the Senior Girls.
