It was one of the biggest days of the DASSA calendar so far. Last week, The Sevens Stadium hosted the DASSA Secondary Cross Country Team Sprint Championships, with more than 1,100 athletes in 240 teams from 48 schools racing across 12 races. Schools competed in two series, Competitive and Development, with U13, U15 and Senior races for boys and girls in each.

## Competitive Series: JESS Arabian Ranches on Top

JESS Arabian Ranches were the team of the day. Their A team was crowned overall Competitive Series champion ahead of Dubai College A, and their B team made it two JESS teams on the podium by taking third.

Consistency won it. JESS Arabian Ranches A finished in the top three in five of the six races, and while they won only one of them, the Senior Girls, they were runners-up in the U13 Boys, U13 Girls, U15 Boys and Senior Boys.

Dubai College A ran them close. They won the U15 Boys, where Dubai College B also took third, and the Senior Boys, where Rugby School Dubai finished third. They added second place in the Senior Girls, ahead of GEMS World Academy, and third in both the U13 and U15 Girls.

Arcadia British School were the other standout, winning both the U13 Girls and the U15 Girls; GEMS Jumeirah College were second in the older race. In the U13 Boys, Safa Community School A took the title, with Royal Grammar School Guildford A in third.

## Development Series: Ambassador International Academy Lead the Way

Ambassador International Academy won the Development Series overall, ahead of Fairgreen International School, with Royal Grammar School Guildford B in third.

Ambassador won the U15 Girls, where Bloom World Academy, GEMS International School and Royal Grammar School Guildford B could not be separated and shared second place. Ambassador also finished second in both Senior races, behind Repton School Dubai in the boys' race and GEMS International School in the girls'.

Fairgreen won the U13 Boys, ahead of Royal Grammar School Guildford B and Dubai British School Jumeira, and finished second in the U15 Boys behind Universal American School, with Arcadia British School B in third. Repton completed a double by also winning the U13 Girls, ahead of Arcadia British School B and Royal Grammar School Guildford B, whose B team reached the podium in three races as well as the overall standings.

The event was organised by JESS Dubai, Super Sports Events and DASSA, with The Sevens Stadium as venue partner, Mediclinic as sponsor and silverware from Winner Sports Trading. Congratulations to every athlete who ran.
