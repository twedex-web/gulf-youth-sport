**Friday saw the cream of the UAE's youth football descend on Jebel Ali School to battle it out in the My Football Cup.**

The event, which was co-ordinated by [M&S Sports](http://mandssports.com/), saw 48 teams from across the region come together in a series of tournaments that ran from the crack of dawn right through to the evening twilight. 

In keeping with the ethos of the groundbreaking [Academy Development League](/news-report-details/7072/FOOTBALL-Youth-League-with-a-Difference), the emphasis of the day was on friendly competition in an unpressurized environment. Parents and coaches were encouraged to let the players learn and develop from their own mistakes; an approach **Mark Gaitskell** (M&S's Managing Director) believes will lead to increased skill levels, game awareness, and crucially, player enjoyment.

As well as the action on the pitch, [Sun and Sand Sports](https://en-ae.sssports.com/) provided some high-octane entertainment on the sidelines in the form of several soccer skills-based challenges, which saw the top performers from the day walk away with a shiny new pair of Nike and Adidas Football Boots.

Although the emphasis was on fun, there were still prizes up for grabs in the tournaments, a list of which can be found below.
