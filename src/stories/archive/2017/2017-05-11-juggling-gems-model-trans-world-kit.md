**Two of GEMS Wellington Silicon Oasis's brightest footballing talents have been celebrated for their juggling skills.**

**Omer** and **Ilyas**, who both won the Trans World Soccer keepy-uppy competition for their respective age-groups at the Dubai School Football League Finals, were presented with a full PUMA match strip, and were given some superstar treatment during a professional photo-shoot.

WSO PE Teacher, **Mark Fennelly**, was delighted for his players:

"Omer and Ilyas have been stand out performers for the WSO school football teams this year. They practice their skills every single day, so it's great to see their hard work and dedication being rewarded."

As well as modelling their new kit for the cameras, the boys also went head to head in a special **Gulf Youth Sport Juggling Challenge**. The details of the challenge are top secret at this stage, but will be revealed in the near future. 

Stay tuned to **gulfyouthsport.com** to ensure you don't miss out!
