This weekend saw the BSME Under 13, Under 15 and Under 19 Netball Tournament hosted by Brighton College Abu Dhabi. All 18 participating schools from around the Middle East performed superbly but there can only be one winning team from each category. The final results were as follows:

Under 13's Winners - The English School Kuwait

Under 15's Winners - The British School Al Khubait, Abu Dhabi

Under 19's Winners - Brighton College Abu Dhabi
