[**Bobby Charlton Soccer & Sports Academy**](http://www.bcssa.co.uk/) [**(BCSSA)**](http://www.bcssa.co.uk/) has announced this week that they will be coming to the Middle East. Bookings are now open for 60 limited places on their camp to be held Friday 24th - Saturday 25th February 2017 in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. They have teamed up with [**Desert Rangers FC Dubai**](https://www.facebook.com/desertrangersfcdubai/) to bring the British coaching expertise to Dubai. David Beckham was discovered at the Bobby Charlton Soccer School and Sir Bobby Charlton then took him straight to Manchester United. David went on to have a fantastic career, which saw him win 6 Premier League titles, the European Cup and captain England.

Bobby Charlton's Academy was the first ever soccer school in the United Kingdom. BCSSA ASIA is an expansion of their coaching services at a global level and this initiative is going to benefit many young football players in the Middle East, with specific emphasis on Dubai this February. “We employ well-qualified, professional coaches who are committed to the Bobby Charlton Soccer & Sports Academy philosophy and are truly exceptional mentors and role models” said Mo Jarad, representative of BCSSA’. “They use their knowledge and expertise to help young players to realize their capabilities and raise their self-esteem” he added. The academy aims to ensure that each and every one of their players enjoys a once in a lifetime experience through this coaching method. For the international students, the school also provides English language lessons along with the football training.

The academy has worked tirelessly for the past 39 years and has had the honor of training and coaching some of the greatest names in the British football history and aims to continue the legacy that was started more than three decades ago. David Beckham is the most celebrated and recognized superstar who was coached and discovered by Bobby Charlton Soccer & Sports Academy. He began his football career as a young boy attending the BCSSA soccer school camps. The overall programs included in the training session carry a plethora of learning and coaching techniques and activities that can be a treasure for the international students and by stepping into the Middle East, BCSSA is all set to tap the new and untapped potential of talent that Dubai has to offer. Manchester United legend Sir Bobby Charlton and David Beckham, are inspirational and videos of David as a young boy can be seen on the BCSSA website.
