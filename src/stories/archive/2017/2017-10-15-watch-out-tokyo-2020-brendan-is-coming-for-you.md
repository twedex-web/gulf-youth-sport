If there’s anyone that can beat you at the breaststroke, it’s probably Brendan Fitzpatrick. The fastest swimmer of his age in the Middle East for breaststroke and individual medley, Brendan is one to watch! He won the French Juniors National this June by swimming the best French performance of all time on the 50 and 100 breaststroke.

He hopes to continue this incredible success for the European and International meets this season and work his way towards the Tokyo Olympics in 2020! Not only a champion in swimming, Brendan is also very academic and is already receiving scholarship offers to top USA universities! Check out the video above for more on this Wonder Kid.
