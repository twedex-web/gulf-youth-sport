Dubai's budding young sprinters are in for a treat at the end of the month with the arrival of British sprint legend, **Joice Maduaka**.

The three-time Olympian, who holds the record for winning the most medals of any athlete at the British National Championships, has teamed up with Dubai-based athletics academy, [Ultimate Athletics](/sports-academy/ultimate-athletics/2205), to put on a series of sprint clinics for teenagers aged 14-17 years.

The available sprint clinic slots are:

Friday 29 September: 8.30am - 11.30am  
Saturday 30 September: 8.30am - 11.30am  
Saturday 30 September: 4.00pm - 7.00pm

The price for the clinic is 250 dhs per person.

To register, email Lisa@ultimateathleticsuae.com
