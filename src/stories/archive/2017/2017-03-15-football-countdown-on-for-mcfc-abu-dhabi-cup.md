The inaugural **Manchester City Abu Dhabi Cup** is now just ten days away, and with more than 800 players across four age-groups battling it out for some incredible prizes, the excitement in the capital is building.

The capital's flagship sporting arena, Zayed Sport City, will play host to the tournament, which is open to Under 10, 12, 14 and 16 sides.

Along with great football on display, the event will also showcase Abu Dhabi as a visitor destination with Etihad Airways and Abu Dhabi Tourism and Culture Authority being key partners.

**What's Up for Grabs...**

The four winning teams will be rewarded with a training camp, delivered by Manchester City coaches, with one of the winning teams will also receiving an unforgettable once-in-a-lifetime trip to the Etihad Campus in Manchester, where they will get to train and experience first-hand, the same facilities as Vincent Kompany, Sergio Aguero and Kevin De Bruyne.

**The best news?.....There's still time to enter!**

If you think your team has what it takes to take the title (and the prize), you can enter by visiting [www.citycupabudhabi.com](http://www.citycupabudhabi.com/)
