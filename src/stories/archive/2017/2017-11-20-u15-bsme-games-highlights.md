The British School Al Khubairat hosted the annual U15 BSME Games in Abu Dhabi this weekend, and the event did not dissappoint.

Spectators were treated to a three-day feast of sporting action, contested by 13 schools from around the GCC.

Well done to all the teams who took part - the Gulf Youth Sport Media Team were blown away by your talent, skill, desire and sportsmanship!

A special mention for the schools who managed to make the podium:

1st - British School Al Khubairat  
2nd - St Christopher's School, Bahrain  
3rd - British International School, Jeddah

To see the full results table, head over to the official [U15 BSME Games website](https://bsmeu15.weebly.com/results.html).
