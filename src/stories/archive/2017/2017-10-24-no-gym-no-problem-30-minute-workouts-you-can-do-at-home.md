**TOP TIP TUESDAY**

Unfortunately, not all of us have enough time each day to make it to the gym. Some of our days are just too jam-packed with responsibilties that we must commit to. Luckily, you don't need a gym to workout! Fitness can be done anywhere and everywhere, all you need is your body, and just about **30 minutes**. You can get just as good a workout in at home with body-weight movements and the use of some furniture as you can at the gym using weights or machines. Ready to break a sweat? Take these workouts with you to the park, your backyard, or even your living room floor, every single day for 30 minutes.

![](http://gulfyouthsport.com/Admin/Content/UserFiles/main_and_5-gettyimages-485933284-monkeybusinessimages.jpg)

**"The Living Room Workout"**

15 jump squats, 5 pushups, 25 high-knees, 7 burpees, 20 alternating lunges, 7 squats, 5 pushups, 20 alternating lunges, 5 pushups, 7 squats, 15 jump squats, 1 minute wall sit, 5 pushups, 25 high-knees

\*Repeat 3-4 times, resting as needed\*

**"Back To Basics"**

50 jumping jacks, 10 squat kickbacks, 50 high knees, 10 alternating lunges, 50 mountain climbers, 10 burpees, 50 jumping jacks, 10 tricep dips (use a chair as an alternative), 50 high knees, 10 pushups, 50 mountain climbers, 10 bicycle crunches

\*Repeat 3-4 times, end with 1 minute plank\*
