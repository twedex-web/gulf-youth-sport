Dubai's newest soccer Academy, City Football Club, officially opened its doors to the public on Saturday afternoon at Kings' Al Barsha.

The pioneering Academy has enlisted some big names, as well as cutting edge technology, in a bid to bridge the gap between the Middle East and collegiate/professional football.

The Gulf Youth Sport media team caught up with Director of Football, Terry Kidd, to find out more about the club's plans.
