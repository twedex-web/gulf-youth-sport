**The DASSA Senior Girls Netball tournament took place at Repton School on Saturday with 29 teams from across Dubai competing for the chance to be crowned the Emirate’s best side.**

Schools competed at U16 and U18 level across seven courts throughout the day, with Dubai College emerging victorious in the cup competition at both age grades on a fantastic day for DC girls netball.

Dubai College pipped Jumeirah English Speaking School to the DASSA title in both cup finals in what was a very commendable effort from the JESS sides.

Wellington International School picked up the plate at U16’s with an impressive win over DESC, while JESS B were victorious in the U18’s plate, beating English College Dubai on their way to lifting the trophy.
