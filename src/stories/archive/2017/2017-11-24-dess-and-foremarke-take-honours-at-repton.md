**The stars of the future descended on Repton School Dubai yesterday for the U8 Boys and U9 Girls Repton Tournaments.**

Sixteen (Under-8) boys and twelve (Under-9) girls teams from around Dubai were in attendance, along with former Manchester United and France star, Mikael Silvestre.

The Gulf Youth Sport Media Team were in attendence to capture action shots from another fantastic event, hosted by the handsome and talented Repton PE teachers. Click [**here**](http://www.gysphoto.com/p376087358) to check out the full gallery.

**Results:**

**U9 Girls Cup Final**  
DESS 1 - 0 Foremarke  
**U9 Girls Plate Final**  
Repton 1 - 1 Safa British School (Safa won on penalties)

**U8 Boys Cup Final**  
Nord Anglia 0 - 0 Foremarke (Foremarke won on penalties)  
**U8 Boys Plate Final**  
Safa Community School 1 - 0 Kings Nad Al Sheba

**Player of the Tournament**  
**Girls:** Tilly Thompson (Foremarke)  
**Boys:** Micah Smith (Nord Anglia)
