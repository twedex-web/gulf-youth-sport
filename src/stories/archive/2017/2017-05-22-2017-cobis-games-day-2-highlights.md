**Highlights from the second day of the 2017 COBIS Games saw the athletes take to the track.**

A stunning performance from BSAK athlete Timi in the 200m helped his team take top spot in the Athletics event on the second day of the COBIS Games.

Dubai College backed up their victory in the pool with a second place finish on the track, meaning the two schools were neck and neck at the top of the overall standings going into the final event of the weekend, football.
