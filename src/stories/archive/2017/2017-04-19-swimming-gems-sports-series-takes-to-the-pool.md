**The final event on the GEMS Sports Series calendar kicked off this morning at the awe-inspiring Hamdan Sports Complex, with over 250 girls from 17 different GEMS schools competing in a host of swimming races.**

With thousands of athletes having already taken part in a host of events - including triathlon, football, and athletics - it's safe to say that the Series, organised by [East Sports Management](http://www.esportsuae.com/), has been a monumental success this year. 

The Gulf Youth Sport Media Team were there to capture the best of the action - [check out the action shots here](/Photos/10135/news/SWIMMING-GEMS-Sports-Series-Takes-to-the-Pool).
