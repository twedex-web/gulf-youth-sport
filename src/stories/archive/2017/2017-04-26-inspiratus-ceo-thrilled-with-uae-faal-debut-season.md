**Du La Liga High Performance Centre clinched the first ever UAE FAAL title with an emphatic victory over closest rivals, Al Ain FC, at The Sevens on Saturday April 22nd.**

The Gulf Youth Sport team caught up with Inspiratus CEO, **Hussein Murad**, and HPC head coach, **Rafael Gil**, after the match to get their take on the UAE FAAL's debut season, as well as the future plans for the HPC players.
