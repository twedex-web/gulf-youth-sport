**It was a sea of red at The Sevens last weekend, as junior gunners from around the Gulf descended on Dubai for the annual Arsenal Soccer School Middle East Tournament.**

The competition, now in its third year, was organised by former Arsenal midfielder, **Ian Selley**, and saw 32 teams from across the Middle East come together for a two-day footballing extravaganza.
