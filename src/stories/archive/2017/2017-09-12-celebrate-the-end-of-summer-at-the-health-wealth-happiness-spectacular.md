Residents of Dubai can celebrate the beginning of cooler temperatures and a more active, outdoor daily life with a holistic lifestyle event for adults and children alike!

**Health, Wealth and Happiness** at **Fitness First**, **The Meadows** taking place on **Friday 15th September** (**1pm-4pm**), will see some of the region’s best brands under each banner including Vita Coco, Float:UAE and Friends Provident, unite for a fun-filled day of activities.  

Attendees of the event spearheaded by financial planning firm Guardian Wealth Management, can get stuck into pursuits such as Salsa and Reggaeton dance lessons hosted by DJ Tolentino; Flowground hoola hoop workshops; Brazilian Capoeira; and the region’s newest fitness craze, BogaFit aka HITT workouts on water!

Additionally sights-to-see will include life coaching, healthy food and drinks, face painting, a chiropractor and more.

Entry to the Health, Wealth and Happiness Spectacular is free but places are limited. Attendees can pre-book entry and the first 50 families through the door will receive a GWM goodie bag! To register head to: [https://www.guardianwealthmanagement.com/blog/health-wealth-and-happiness](https://www.guardianwealthmanagement.com/blog/health-wealth-and-happiness)
