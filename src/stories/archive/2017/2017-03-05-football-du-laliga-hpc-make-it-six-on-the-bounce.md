**du LaLiga HPC Achieves Record 6-match Winning Run in du UAE FA Academy League**

**du LaLiga HPC** maintained a 6-week winning run in the **du UAE FAAL League**, after defeating **E-Sports** on home turf at the Sevens. In the most eagerly anticipated matches of week 6, **E-Sports** were well organised but failed to interrupt **du LaLiga HPC’s** hot winning streak.  **du LaLiga HPC** star **Ahmed Arif** opened the scoring on the 5-minute mark, latching on to a fantastic cross by **Ahmed Salam**. **Arif** almost made it 2 when he was put through by **Rajeh**, but **E-Sports** goalkeeper **Umar Zahid** made a brilliant save.

In the second half, **E-Sports** came back strong, led by the experience of Coach Terry Kidd, attacking the **du LaLiga HPC** backline at every opportunity. **du LaLiga HPC** wingers **Hallal** and **Salam** were a constant threat for the home side in the first half but it was clear E-Sports had adapted tactically in the second half to combat their threat. With twenty minutes to go **E-Sports** launched a series of attacks on the du LaLiga HPC goal, but goalkeeper **Wahdan** stood strong with a string of first class saves. Moments before the final whistle, a superb pass from **Hallal** allowed **Matni** to hit the back of the net once more, which led du LaLiga HPC to a 2-0 win and pinned **E-Sports** back to 4th position in the League table.

Over at Al Ain on Thursday, **Al Ain Club** maintained their winning streak and reclaimed 2nd place in the League table, thanks to 4 goals from **David Guel** and additional 3 goals from his team members, which landed them a 7-0 win against **Legends**, with still two games at hand.

Three matches kicked off Friday afternoon, the first taking place in Ras Al Khaimah between **Emirates Club** and **FCBEscola**.  In a heated match, the boys from **FCBEscola** took the win 3-1, which helped them attain 5th place in the table.

In Abu Dhabi, **Abu Dhabi Elite** put on an impressive display against Dubai’s **PSG** finishing the match with a superb 4-0 win and moving up to take 3rd place in the League Table. 

In another thrilling match, **Al Jazira Club** won 3-1 over **EFF** from Abu Dhabi with 2 goals in the last 2 minutes of the match.

Dubai’s **Elite** **FC** and **TFA** played a tough match on Saturday with **TFA** opening the score-line with a penalty 6 minutes into the match and continuing strong to take the win at 3-1.
