Ultimate Athletics penultimate Race Night of the season took place in perfect conditions on Thursday evening, with hundreds of young athletes taking to the track in their quest for personal bests.

This was the 5th of 6 race nights, with each athlete's points throughout the season accumulating, meaning athletes are rewarded for perseverance and consistency.
