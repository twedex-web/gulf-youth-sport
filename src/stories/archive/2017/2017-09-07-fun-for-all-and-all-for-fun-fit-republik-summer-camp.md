**Fit Republik Summer Camp**

With summer coming to an end, this means that the 2-month long Fit Republik Kids Summer Camp has come to a close as well. However, the little ones will be returning back to school with smiles on their faces with the memories of an activity-packed summer.

With the slogan “Fun for all and all for fun”, Fit Republik, the largest sports center in the Middle East had over 3,000 kids join the 2017 summer camp. To say it was a success, would be an understatement. 

The summer camp ran from July 2nd until August 31st and children from ages 5-14 were welcome to join the entertaining camp with rates starting from 250 AED to 960 AED per week.

To ensure there wasn’t a single dull moment, the coordinators made sure the kids had a variety of different and exciting activities to do throughout the summer. Some of these activities consisted of things like; Crossfit Kids, Aquatics “The Wibit”, Gymnastics, Flag Football, Obstacle courses, Arts and Crafts, and many, many more.

When asked about their experience at the summer camp, the youngsters were jolting with excitement. They raved about all the fun activities they got to take part in with all the new friends they made. The Aquatics “Wibit” seemed to be a widely popular one for the kiddos. But then again, who wouldn’t like jumping around on a massive inflatable obstacle course on top of a swimming pool?

As for the upcoming school year, to make sure the kids stay fit all year-round, Fit Republik will be offering a variety of fitness classes for kids at all levels, such as; Crossfit Kids, Martial Arts, Aquatics, Gymnastics, and many more. The classes will be commencing on September 17th, and will run throughout the entire school year.

Head over to the juniors section of the [Fit Republik](https://www.fitrepublik.com/category/kids-activities/) website to find out more about their exciting upcoming U18 programmes.
