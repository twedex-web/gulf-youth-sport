Take a look behind the scenes of the BSME Awards ceremony. The champions of the three-day event were The British School Al Khubairat who got gold for boys volleyball and boys football. St Christopher’s School got second place for their outstanding results in athletics and boys football. Third place went to British International School Jeddah for their excellent performance in Football and Athletics. Huge congratulations to all participants!
