Certainly, there is absolutely nothing wrong with enjoying some delicious pizza every now and then. It is completely okay to indulge in some “unhealthy” foods once in a while, just as long as it’s in moderation and doesn’t become a habit! With that being said, we all love healthy food that tastes so good it makes you question whether it is actually healthy or not. Here are a few delicious must-try healthy restaurants/cafes in Dubai. 

**1.Nathalie’s** 

Located at Fit Republik in Sports City, Nathalie’s menu consists of nothing but mouthwateringly delicious guilt-free meals. With their slogan being “Simple food. Real food. Honest food”, their menu consists of meals like “Gluten Free Blueberry Parfait”, “Berry Smoothie Bowl”, and “Grilled Salmon Fillet and Gluten Free Veggie Soba Noodles”. They offer tons of raw-food options, as well as healthy low-carb meals.

![](http://gulfyouthsport.com/Admin/Content/UserFiles/finalll.jpg)

**2\. Common Grounds**

Located in Mall of The Emirates & JBR, Common Grounds offers a unique menu incredible breakfast options (the Avocado On Toast & Acai Bowl is right up there with the very best), healthy lunch options, pastries, and delicious coffee for all you caffeine lovers out there. With a very cosy, rustic-modern like interior, common grounds is a very warm and happy atmosphere.

![](http://gulfyouthsport.com/Admin/Content/UserFiles/this.jpg)

**3\. Pineapple Express**

Located in JLT Almas Tower & The Acai Spot is the perfect place to go if you’re craving a delicious and refreshing healthy dessert. Their Acai Bowls and raw "Snickers” bars, gluten free waffles, as well as fresh smoothies are the talk of the town at the moment. Pineapple express is a cafe with a special emphasis on super foods and everything that is “good for your insides”. They also offer a full breakfast menu, “fitness fanatic” meals, and lots more.
