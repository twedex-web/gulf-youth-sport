**Seventeen schools from across the world have descended on the UK to battle it out in the 2017 COBIS Primary Games.**

The three day sporting extravaganza will be hosted at Stantonbury in Milton Keynes, England. Day one sees the athletes compete for honours in the pool. Athletics follows on day two, with football rounding things off on day three.

Representing the Middle East are:

-   The British International School, Riyadh (KSA)
-   The British International School, Cairo (Egypt)
-   Jumeirah English Speaking School (UAE)
-   Dubai English Speaking School (UAE)

Good luck to all schools (especially the GCC schools) from everyone at Gulf Youth Sport!
