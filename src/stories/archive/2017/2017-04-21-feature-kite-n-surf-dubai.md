**Our Friday Feature this week focuses on quite possibly the coolest company in Dubai.**

Founded less than a year ago by founded by five water-sports mad brothers, [Kite n' Surf](http://www.kitensurf.ae/) has taken the water-sports industry by storm. 

We visited the guys last weekend at a sun-soaked Kite Beach to find out why they're the go-to company for the Dubai kids looking for a water-based adrenaline rush.

Interested in trying your hand at Standup Paddleboarding, Surfing, or Kite Surfing? Check them out at http://www.kitensurf.ae/
