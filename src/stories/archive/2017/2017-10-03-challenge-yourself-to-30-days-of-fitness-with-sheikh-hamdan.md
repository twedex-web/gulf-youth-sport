Get active in Dubai with thousands of other residents...

* * *

If you’re looking for a new fitness challenge to take on, we think we’ve found just the right one! His Highness Sheikh Hamdan bin Mohammed bin Rashid al Maktoum, Crown Prince of Dubai recently announced his initiative to make Dubai the most active city in the world. The ‘30x30 challenge’ will run from 20th October - 18th November 2017 and aims to get as many people in Dubai as possible to register and take part.

![](http://gulfyouthsport.com/Admin/Content/UserFiles/tmtd3.jpg)

The number of activities you can get involved with seem almost endless and are a great way for people of all ages and abilities to enjoy fitness every day. If you can spare just 30 minutes out of your day, then you can get involved in this incredible movement. The GYS team are registered and looking forward to seeing what the Dubai Fitness Challenge has to offer! To register as an individual, a school or as an organisation, visit [www.dubaifitnesschallenge.com](http://www.dubaifitnesschallenge.com/).
