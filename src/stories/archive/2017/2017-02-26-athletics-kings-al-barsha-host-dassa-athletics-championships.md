**Kings' School Al Barsha** last week hosted the **DASSA Athletics Championships** at Sports City. 

Schools from across Dubai took part in a series of track events including 100m, 200m, 800m, 4 x 100 relay races, and field events including javelin, high jump and shot putt. 

Gulf Youth Sport were there on both days to soak up the atmosphere, speak to the stars of the show and capture the best of the action.
