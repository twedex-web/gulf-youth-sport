**Monday saw the U11 DASSA Touch Rugby Series come to a pulsating climax at Kings' Nad Al Sheba.** The finals of the competition - which was making its debut in the school sports calendar - saw teams from across Dubai battle it out for the final places following two preliminary rounds earlier in the year.

After some fast paced and bruising encounters, it was Dubai English Speaking School who emerged victorious, narrowly edging out Kings' in a thrilling final.
