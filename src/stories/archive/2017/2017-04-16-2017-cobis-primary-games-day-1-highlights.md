**Highlights from the first day of the 2017 COBIS Primary Games in Milton Keynes, where the athletes participated in the opening ceremony as well as the swimming event.** 

The day saw two of Dubai's power-houses, JESS and DESS, battle it out at the top of the leaderboard, with the lead yo-yo'ing between the teams on numerous occasions. JESS eventually edged out their neighbours to take the overall lead going into the second day of competition.

Stay tuned to Gulf Youth Sport this week for more highlights from the event.
