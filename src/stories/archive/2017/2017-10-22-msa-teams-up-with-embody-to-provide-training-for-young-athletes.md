The pool of talent in the UAE when it comes to fitness is exceptional, particularly in the younger generation. With all of this untapped potential, it’s vital that they have somewhere to grow and develop with the help of expert coaching. With the help of highly qualified, educated and certified coaches, the future stars of sports can begin to shine. My Sports Academy provide long-term athlete development programmes for children (and adults) with next-level attention to detail.

In order to give their students a 360 education and training plan, My Sports Academy have partnered with Embody Fitness. Embody’s bespoke equipment and facilities provide a unique space, perfect for nurturing the young athletes - without a treadmill in sight. The gym comes with an elite team of sports professionals, with olympic backgrounds, making it the perfect pairing for the Academy’s athletes.
