The Abu Dhabi Youth League came to a thrilling finale in the capital yesterday.

Regional Sports defeated Champions Academy in a closely fought battle to lift the U10 Division 2 title, whilst in a more tactical encounter PASS Masters U10 were pipped by City Football Schools (5-4) on penalties after a 0-0 dra in normal time.

Our roving reporter Stuart Birkwood captured some fantastic shots from the day where, above all, football was the winner.
