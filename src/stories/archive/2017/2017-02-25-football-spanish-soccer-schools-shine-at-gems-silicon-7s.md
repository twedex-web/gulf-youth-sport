Hundreds of budding footballers descended on Dubai this weekend for the The GEMS Silicon 7's. 

The tournament, run by **ESports Football Club**, played host to 40 teams from three different age groups at GEMS Wellington Academy in Silicon Oasis.

The U6 Tournament was a five-a-side format and all matches were end to end from start to finish. The Final was a no different and, despite **Spanish Soccer Schools** edging a 1-0 win over **Soccer Kids Destroyers** with a late goal from **Simon**, it could have been either side's game. The Spaniards also ran out winners in the seven-a-side U8 category, overcoming **EFC Suarez** 5-2 with goals from **Ryan (2), Woody, Gabriel, Adriane and Lewis**.

Julio, coach at **Spanish Soccer Schools**, was proud of his young players, "We are teaching children to become clever footballers with a focus on technical skills and ball mastery. The U8's were especially impressive today, they have improved every week since returning from the holidays!"

**Manchester City Soccer Schools** were comfortable winners at U10 defeating Pro Sports Academy 4-0 with goals from Mido Said (2), Ahmed Hamed and Zack Toye.
