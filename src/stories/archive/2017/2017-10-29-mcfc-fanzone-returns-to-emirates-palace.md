**Abu Dhabi based Man City fanatics are in for a treat next weekend with the return of the MCFC FanZone at Emirates Palace on Sunday 5th November.**  

Families and Manchester City FC fans alike from across the UAE will be able to come together and enjoy a FREE fun-filled, family afternoon at the Cityzens FanZone where they will be able to watch Manchester City FC go head to head with English Premier League side Arsenal. 

The FanZone event in Abu Dhabi will be held at Emirates Palace’s Marina Pitch, from 17:30 and kick-off at 18:15.

Activities and competitions will take place before the game and at half-time with refreshments available throughout the day.

**Date:** Sunday 5th November

**Time:** Doors open at 17:30 and kick-off at 18:15.

**Location:** Emirates Palace, Marina Pitch

**Parking:** Limited parking will be available at the Marina Car Park and will be allocated on first come first served basis.

**Price:** FREE with everyone welcome

**Register:** Fans can register by going to [mancity.com/officialscreeningevent](http://mancity.com/officialscreeningevent)
