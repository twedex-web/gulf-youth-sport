**The boys from Kings' Al Barsha sprinted to an awe-inspiring victory in the final event of the BSAK Under 13 BSME Games.**

The Kings' boys, coached and mentored by DASSA Athletics Coordinator, **Colin Morris**, combined scintilating pace with slick change-overs to decimate the field, eventually winning by a margin of 9-metre over nearest rivals, BSAK.

We caught up with the team straight after the race to get their reactions to a massive victory for such a small school.
