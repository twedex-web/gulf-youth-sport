The Dubai Intercontinental Cup U13 2017 saw some of the biggest names in football (AC Milan, Real Madrid, and Celta Vigo) battle it out with teams from the UAE, as well as teams from as far afield as Australia and Japan.

Despite an early defeat to AFA Angola, Celta Vigo emerged as the champions of the tournament, crowning their victory with a thrilling 1-0 win over Real Madrid in the final.
