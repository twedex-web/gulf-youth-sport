In an age where young sporting talents face ever-increasing scrutiny both on and off the field, the ability to handle the Media is an essential skill.

With this in mind, Gulf Youth Sport teamed up with Dubai English Speaking College (where there is no shortage of talent) to treat some of their future stars to a Sports Media Training Master-class.
