Gulf Youth Sport are delighted to be the media partners of the [Emirates NBD Unity Run](http://www.unityrun.ae/). The annual run celebrates the UAE’s diversity and inclusion of people from all over, with all levels of abilities. Offering a choice between a 3km walk or 10km run, the event will be held on 24th November at Dubai Silicon Oasis. It aims to celebrate the inclusiveness of the UAE and Dubai’s commitment to promoting access for persons with disabilities in all areas of life.

The Emirates NBD Unity Village, alongside The Emirates NBD Unity Run is a mix of entertainment, retail and food and beverage outlets. It attracts participants, supporters and spectators enjoying the carnival atmosphere at the stunning pineapple building in Dubai Silicon Oasis.

The Emirates NBD Unity Run raises awareness for persons with Disabilities supporting Dubai’s initiative to be a disability friendly city by 2020. GYS are proud to be a part of this great community event and are looking forward to capturing all the action from the day.
