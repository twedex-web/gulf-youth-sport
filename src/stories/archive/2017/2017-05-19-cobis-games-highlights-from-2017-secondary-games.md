The **COBIS Secondary School Games** were staged in Sheffield on Friday 19th - Sunday 21st May, 2017. This video showcases highlights from day 1 which saw both girls and boys take to the pool.

**[Dubai College](http://www.dubaicollege.org/)** finished day in first place with a stunning total of 270 points, and were joined on the podium by **[British School of Al Khubairat](http://www.britishschool.sch.ae/)** in second with 260 points, and **[Doha College](http://www.dohacollege.com/)** in third with 250 points respectfully. 

British International School of Riyadh fininshed fourth, New Cairo British International School in sixth, British School Bahrain in seventh, British International School of Cairo in eleventh meaning all seven Middle-East schools finished in the top half of the twenty four strong field.
