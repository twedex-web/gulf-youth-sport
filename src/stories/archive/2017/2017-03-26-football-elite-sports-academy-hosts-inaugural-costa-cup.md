Friendly football and irresistible coffee...what's not to love? **[Elite Sports Academy](http://elitesports.ae/)** hosted the inaugural **[Costa](http://www.costacoffee.ae/)** Cup staged at **[Horizon International School](http://www.horizoninternationaldubai.com/)** in Dubai.

Over 100 recreation players had the chance to play competitive football in a fun and controlled environment. 

**Craig Curtis**, Operations Manager at Elite Sports Academy, said: "The whole day was a huge success - it's important that our recreation players have the chance to show their skills in a competitive environment. Thanks to Costa for supporting the event - we hope to run similar tournaments in the future and provide even more opportunities for our young players."
