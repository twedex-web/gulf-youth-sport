**Dubai's netballers will be back in action on the 16th of September with the annual, pre-season, Versatility Tournament.**

The event, hosted at The Sevens and organised by the [Dubai Netball League](http://dubainetballleague.com/), invites players to sign up on the day as an individual - each player will then be allocated to a team. Every player will play every position to promote versatility and to give any new players a taste of everything netball has to offer.

The entrance fee for each player is 120dhs, with the youth tournament open to girls aged 10-14, and the senior tournament open to players aged 14+.

There will be prizes for the Player of the Tournament and each player of the winning team PLUS free gifts for all (to be announced soon).

For more information, contact the organisers at: tournaments.dnl@gmail.com
