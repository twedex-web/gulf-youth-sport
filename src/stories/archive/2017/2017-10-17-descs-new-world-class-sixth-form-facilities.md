In a bid to bridge the gap between GCSE and higher education, Dubai English Speaking College have transformed their Sixth Form facilities. The new space, situated on the main school campus in Academic City, is a modern, fun space designed with creativity and education in mind.

Within this new building, comes a world-class gym, complete with technology that allows each student to have a personally designed training programme. DESC want to ensure that each student can fulfil their potential, no matter what their abilities. Additionally there’s a full size international grass pitch, netball courts and a soon-to-open indoor swimming pool.

Andrew Gibbs, Principal of Dubai English Speaking School and Dubai English Speaking College (DESC) said “This is a breathtaking addition to the current DESC campus, and with the clear feel of a bespoke Sixth Form College, it promises to provide a unique new platform for post-16 students in Dubai”.

With the best interests of our students always at the very heart of our continuing development, this innovative expansion will further enhance our vision of making DESC a genuinely world-class provider of UK secondary education.
