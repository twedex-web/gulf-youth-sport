**Full highlights of the 2017 UAE Teen Sports and Fitness Awards, which were held at Arabian Ranches Golf Club on April 28.** 

The event, organised by [FITTGroup Middle East](http://www.fittgroupme.com/)'s managing partners, Ruth Dickinson and Danil Bornventure, celebrates the achievements of the rising stars of UAE sport, as well as the coaches and organisations who are helping them reach their potential.
