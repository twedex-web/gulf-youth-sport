**Arsenal Soccer School Kuwait emerged as the pride of the nation as they secured three stunning victories at the annual Arsenal Soccer School tournament.**

The competition, which was held at The Sevens in Dubai and organised by former Arsenal midfielder, **Ian Selley**, saw 32 teams from across the Gulf region come together for a two-day footballing extravaganza.

The nine Kuwaiti squads that had travelled to the UAE for the event were cheered on by a boisterous group of fans throughout the competition. They did not dissappoint their fans, as by the end of the weekend, the Kuwaiti Gunners had lifted the Cup in the Under-10, Under-12, and Under-14 age-groups. 

For Head Coach, **Hannah Farmer**, the victories mark the culmination of five years of hard work and dedication from both staff and players:

_"Many of the children that are here this weekend have been with us right from the start (2011) - it's been an amazing journey and I'm indescribably proud of all of them."_

We spoke to Hannah and her rising stars following the presentation to get their reaction.
