**As the weather heats up, City Football Schools will head indoors across their three venues for their fifth term of the season and will be offering specific Ramadan timings throughout the Holy Month.**

Ramadan sessions will run on both Mondays and Wednesdays from 20:00 – 22:00 at the Mubadala Arena, Zayed Sports City to make it more convenient for children to exercise and for their families to take them to their sessions.

In addition to the added Ramadan sessions on offer, Term 5 will also see week day sessions taking place at New York University Abu Dhabi and Emirates Palace earlier in the evening. 

As part of City Football Schools ongoing commitment to the development of young footballers in the region, they will also be hosting a variety of Summer Camps throughout July for children aged 6-12 years old.  The sessions will give both boys and girls the opportunity to improve their football skills, stay healthy, make friends and have lots of fun in a positive and friendly learning environment all under the guidance and supervision of fully qualified City Football coaches.

All players who sign up for Term 5 will receive a 10% discount on the Summer Camps.

**Full Details**

**Dates:** 13 May – 28 June

**Venues:**

·       Mubadala Arena, Zayed Sports City

·       New York University Abu Dhabi

·       Emirates Palace, Corniche

\*Ramadan timings available Monday and Wednesday from 20:00 – 22:00 at the Mubadala Arena, Zayed Sports City. 

**Booking Promotion:**  All players that sign up for Term 5 will receive a 10% discount on the Summer Camps. 

**Prices:** From AED 900 + VAT 

**Age:** Boys and girls, 3 – 18 years’ old and all abilities
