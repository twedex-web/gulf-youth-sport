**The GEMS Sports Series Athletics Championships kicked off in style yesterday at GEMS Modern Academy, Dubai.**

With over 18 GEMS schools in attendance from across the UAE, the competition was feirce, and the standard on show was phenomenal.

After an action packed morning on the track and in the field, hosts **GEMS Modern Academy** took victory in the Boys competition, whilst the girls in green from **Jumeirah Primary School** reined supreme in their division.

The Gulf Youth Sport Media Team were in attendance throughout the event to capture these incredible young athletes in action - [click here to see the full album](http://www.gysphoto.com/p739259621).
