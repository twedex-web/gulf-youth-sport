**Good news for Dubai's dance enthusiasts; Dubai's premiere Dance Academy, [Sharmila Dance](http://sharmiladance.com/), are hosting an entire afternoon of FREE classes to celebrate the launch of their new dance centre.**

Sharmila is understandably keen to show off the bespoke dance studios, which occupy the bulk of the third floor of [Al Attar Business Centre](https://www.google.ae/maps/place/Al+Attar+Business+Centre/@25.1089153,55.1837495,15z/data=!4m5!3m4!1s0x0:0x74e19804f606bf8!8m2!3d25.1089153!4d55.1837495), Al Barsha 1, and is generously offering dancers of all ages and abilities the opportunity to take part in a free class of their choice tomorrow (Saturday 31st August) afternoon.

The centre will be open from 12noon, with classes starting at 2pm - full details can be found at Sharmila's [website](http://sharmiladance.com/) and [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/sharmiladance/) page.
