**In partnership with City Football Schools, we are giving you the chance to win a space for your team at the [Manchester City Abu Dhabi Cup](https://www.citycupabudhabi.com/en/), 23rd - 24th March 2018.**

There are only four spaces remaining for the second edition of the Abu Dhabi Cup, and Gulf Youth Sport have two places to give away!

For your chance to win this amazing prize, simply enter your details below and share the post on the Gulf Youth Sport [Facebook page](http://facebook.com/GulfYouthSport2).

Each unique submission counts towards your chances of winning this highly sought-after prize, so make sure you share with team mates, coaches and parents to give your team the best chance of winning!

Competition closes at 5pm (UAE time) on Saturday 17th February 2018, winners will be announced on Sunday 18th February 2018, Ts & Cs apply.
