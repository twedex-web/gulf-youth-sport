**Dubai Sports City has announced its popular family fete is back for a ‘Spring Fete’ edition, which will take place on Saturday 14th April.** Families can enjoy a FREE fun-filled afternoon at Dubai Sports City’s Canal Residence West’s Retail Promenade before the weather gets too hot outside!  

With fantastic raffle prizes to be won, lots of children’s activities including play areas, bouncy castles, face painting, arts and crafts, games as well as storytelling, quizzes and competitions, Dubai Sports City its really place to be!

There will also be event day discounts and prizes from participating Canal Residence West’s outlets.

**Date:** Saturday 14th April 2018  

**Time:** 16:00 – 20:00pm

**Location:** Dubai Sports City’s Canal Residence West’s Retail Promenade

**Price:** FREE

For more information about ‘Spring Fete’ or any of Dubai Sports City’s facilities please visit: [www.dsc.ae](http://www.dsc.ae/) or call 04 425 1111
