**Welsh and British and Irish Lions star Mike Phillips will be hosting a number of FREE kids rugby coaching sessions this weekend as part of his recently launched Mike Phillips Just Play Rugby Academy.**

The sessions will be open to children aged 5 – 14 and take place at Nord Anglia International School this Friday 26th January, from 9am.

Children taking part in the sessions will not only be able to learn from Mike and a number of experienced coaches but will enhance their running, chasing, passing and dodging skills and also build on their social skills with a number of team building exercises.

To sign up for your FREE lesson or find out more information visit - [https://www.facebook.com/events/352136671926887/](https://www.facebook.com/events/352136671926887/)

**Location:** Nord Anglia International School

**Date:** Friday 26th January

Under 7 & Under 8 10:00 - 11:00  
Under 9 & Under 10 10:00 - 11:00
