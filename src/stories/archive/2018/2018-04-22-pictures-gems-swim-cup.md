The 2018 GEMS Swim Cup, organised by ESM in conjunction with technical partners Hamilton Aquatics, took place last week in Dubai's stunning Hamdan Sports Complex.

The event saw over 24 schools from across the United Arab Emirates, as well as one from Nairobi, Kenya, come together to battle it out in the breathtaking arena.

A full list of results will be posted shortly.
