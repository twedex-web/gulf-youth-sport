**The next generation of Abu Dhabi's football stars got the chance to meet their heroes when English Premier League champions-elect Manchester City jetted in to the UAE for a warm-weather training camp ahead of the final push in this season's domestic and European competitions.** 

Manchester City – who’s youth development programme City Football Schools are based in Abu Dhabi – have been drawn against Liverpool in the last eight of the Champions League and are well on course to secure their first Premier League title under Pep Guardiola.

The City squad took time out of their busy schedule to pose with local fans and aspiring players, giving the youngsters the chance to grab selfies, autographs and take in advice from some of the best players on the planet.
