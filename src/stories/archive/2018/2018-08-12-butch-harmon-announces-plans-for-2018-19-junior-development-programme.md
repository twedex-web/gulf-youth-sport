**The Butch Harmon Dubai has announced that their nine-week Junior development programme for players between of 3 to 15 years is back for a new term which will commence on Sunday 30th September.**

Young, aspiring golfers will receive training from the best coaches with the world class facilities at Dubai Sports City.

The new programme caters to various levels and is spread across three 9-week terms which will ensure that each player is able to develop their skills in a fun, creative and enjoyable environment. The programme will include a coaching session, a supervised practice session and a session on the course each week.

With a revised fee structure for each program, the fundamentals level of the program for beginners aged 5-15 years works out as inexpensive as AED 1,100 per term and the active start level for kids aged 3 and 4 years is priced at an affordable AED870 per term.

The first nine-week term of the programme will commence on Sunday 30th September and will take place until the 8th December 2018.

For more information on the Junior Development Programme please contact 04 425 1040 or email [reservation@butchharmondubai.com](mailto:reservation@butchharmondubai.com)

**_To find out more about Dubai Sports City please visit:_** [http://www.dsc.ae/](http://www.dsc.ae/)
