Denver Nuggets' ace, [Mason Plumlee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mason_Plumlee), has arrived in Dubai ahead of the Duplays Hoops Summit, which kicks off at Aflec School in Dubai on Tuesday 28th August.

The former Duke Blue Devils be in town for a total of 4-days, where he will host the 'Mason Plumlee Basketball Camp', as well as a series of 3 x 3 Youth Tournaments.

To find out more, check out the [dedicated event page on Duplays website](https://duplays.com/leagues/dubai/basketball/8474)
