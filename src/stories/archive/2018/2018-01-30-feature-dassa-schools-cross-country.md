Two rounds down, one to go.

With just one race left in the Kibson's Schools X-Country league, it's still all to play for.

We caught up with event organiser Andy Toward, Head of PE at Rashid School for Boys, to find out more about one of Dubai's biggest, best and toughest school sports events.

The third and final race in the series will take place on February 5th (primary schools) and February 6th (secondary schools).

Head to the [Super Sports UAE](http://www.supersportsuae.com/races/kibsons-schools-x-country-league/) website for more details.
