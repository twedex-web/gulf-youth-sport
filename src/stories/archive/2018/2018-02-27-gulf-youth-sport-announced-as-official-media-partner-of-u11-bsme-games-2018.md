**Gulf Youth Sport is delighted to announce its position as Official Media Partner for the BSME U11 Large School Games at Dubai’s Kings’ School Al Barsha this week.**![](http://gulfyouthsport.com/Admin/Content/UserFiles/bsme-logo.png)

The event will see 16 schools from across the Middle East travel to the UAE in a bid to be crowned 2018 Champions. The event includes five sports; athletics, swimming, football, boys’ basketball and girls’ netball, all to be hosted at Kings’ Al Barsha.

Gulf Youth Sport will be providing video highlights, exclusive interviews, photos and news from the event which runs from Thursday 1st March to Saturday 3rd March.

The schools competing in the BSME U11 Large School Games are as follows:

-   Kings' School Al Barsha
-   St Christopher's, Bahrain
-   Kings' School Dubai
-   The British International School of Jeddah
-   Al Yasmina Academy
-   Dubai English Speaking School
-   British International School Cairo
-   British School Muscat
-   British International School Riyadh
-   British International School Abu Dhabi
-   British School Al Khubairat
-   Brighton College Abu Dhabi
-   Modern English School Cairo
-   El Alsson British and American international School
-   Cairo English School
-   Azzan Bin Qais International School

For more information about the event visit the 2018 BSME U11 Large School Games website [here](http://bsmeu11large.weebly.com/).
