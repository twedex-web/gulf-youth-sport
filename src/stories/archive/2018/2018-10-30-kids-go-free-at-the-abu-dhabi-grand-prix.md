The next generation of racing fans can experience the sights, sounds, thrills and spills of the FORMULA 1 2018 ETIHAD AIRWAYS ABU DHABI GRAND PRIX for free on Friday, November 23.

The year’s most eagerly awaited weekend of racing is a true family affair with up to four children under the age of 12 able to accompany an adult for free on Friday 23 November with each three-day adult Grandstand ticket purchased.

That’s not all, if you have purchased a Super Parks Pass, the Kids Go Free offer will also extend to Yas Island Parks – Ferrari World Abu Dhabi, Yas Waterworld andWarner Bros World™ Abu Dhabi, meaning there’s even more fun to be hand on the world famous Yas Island.

This year’s Kids Go Free package includes:

-   Access to Grandstand ‘free seating’ on Friday for all children under 12 accompanied by an adult with a valid ticket
-   Access to all entertainment areas within the Circuit including the F1® Fanzone and Oasis Areas
-   F1® Fanzone and Oasis Area entertainment will include family friendly entertainment
-   Free entry to Yas Island Parks for Super Parks Pass holders – Ferrari World Abu Dhabi,  Yas Waterworld and Warner Bros World™ Abu Dhabi for all children under 12 years

To access Kids Go Free day **registration is essential** and must be completed before 22nd November 2018 via the Yas Marina Circuit website: [https://www.yasmarinacircuit.com/en/formula-1-tickets/kids-go-free/kids-go-free-register/](https://www.yasmarinacircuit.com/en/formula-1-tickets/kids-go-free/kids-go-free-register/)

Access cannot be granted to those that arrive on the day without prior registration.

Existing F1® ticket holders can also upgrade their ticket to a Super Parks Pass, which allows unlimited access to all three Yas Island Parks on Wednesday through to Sunday, and can be purchased via Yas Marina Circuit Call Centre (800 927) or +971 (0) 2 659 9800.

Tickets are available online at [www.yasmarinacircuit.com](http://www.yasmarinacircuit.com/), through the Yas Marina Circuit Call Centre (800 927) or +971 (0) 2 659 9800, as well as at Etihad Shops and Virgin Megastores, and through the YasHUB smartphone app and its Facebook shop: [www.facebook.com/yasmarinacircuit](http://www.facebook.com/yasmarinacircuit)
