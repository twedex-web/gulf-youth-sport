**The British Olympians are coming! For your chance to win a FREE place at next week's Great Britain Athletics Academy, held at Kings' School Nad Al Sheba Sunday 4th March - Thursday 8th March, complete the form below by 5pm (UAE time) on Wednesday 28th February 2018.**

Each unique entry counts towards the draw, duplicate entries will not be counted.

All unsuccessful entries will qualify for a 100dhs discount upon booking a place on the five-day camp.

For more information on the Great Britain Athletics Academy, visit their website [here](http://athletesinschools.ae/athleticsacademy/).
