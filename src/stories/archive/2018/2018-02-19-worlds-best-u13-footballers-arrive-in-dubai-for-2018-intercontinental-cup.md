**The world's brightest young footballers have arrived in Dubai ahead of the second edition of Intercontinental Cup.**

Dubai Sports City will again play host to the high profile tournament, with Real Madrid legend Michel Salgado and his team at Spanish Soccer Schools calling the shots.

This year sees some even bigger names than in 2017, the most notable of which is Real's arch rivals, FC Barcelona. 

Sports fans looking to catch a glimpse of the action can attend the matches (9am - 9pm), or tune into Dubai Sports TV for the live stream.

Gulf Youth Sport will also be in attendance, bringing you a round up of the best of the action from what is sure to be a thrilling three days of high calibre football.
