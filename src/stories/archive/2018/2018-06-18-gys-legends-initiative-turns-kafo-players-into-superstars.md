**Freddie Kanoute may have become accustomed to cameras flashing in his face during his illustrious playing career, but it was a whole new experience for the KAFO Academy players last month, as they became the first Sports Academy to become GYS Legends.**

After posing for the cameras, the GYS Media Team got to work in the editing studio to produce the stunning [final product](http://www.gysphoto.com/f127951182).

The free initiative has been set up by Gulf Youth Sport to give local children a chance to experience life in the spotlight, and some valuable media training in case they follow in their coach's foot-steps and make it to the big time! 

Sports Academies taking part in GYS Legends initiative will also receive a selection of images to use on their website and social media platforms, whilst parents have the option to purchase prints/downloadable images of their very own superstars direct from GYS.

**To book in your GYS Legends photo-shoot, get in touch with the GYS Team via [email](mailto:info@gulfyouthsport.com), or phone +971 43 686 510.**
