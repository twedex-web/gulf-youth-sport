**Hamilton Aquatics swimmer, Lewis Burras (Team GB) has a busy evening ahead at the European Junior Championships in Helsinki.**   
Lewis is through to the Semi Final (2nd fastest) of the 50m Freestyle at 18:00 UAE time. If everything goes to plan he will be in the Final at 19:15 UAE time.   
Lewis is also in the final of the 4 x 100 Medley Relay at 20:08 UAE time, where Great Britain will be racing for a medal against some of the fastest swimmers in the world!  
Yesterday Lewis picked up a Silver Medal for swimming the heat of the Mixed Medley Relay - Well done Lewis, we are extremely proud.   
**Francesca Falzon-Young** (Team Malta) has had a successful meet, finishing in her highest ever placing at European Juniors and being on most of her best times - Well done Francesca!
