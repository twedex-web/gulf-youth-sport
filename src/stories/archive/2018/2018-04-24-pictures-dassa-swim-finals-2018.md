Over 500 of the best young swimmers in Dubai descended on the Hamdan Sports Complex yesterday (23.04.2018) for the 2018 DASSA Swim Finals.

With over 35 schools in attendence, the standard of competition was fierce, with records tumbling throughout the day.

The Gulf Youth Sport media team were on hand to capture the action - check out the event [highlights](https://www.facebook.com/gulfyouthsport2/videos/948639321981900/) here, and check out the pictures [here](http://www.gysphoto.com/p307211810).
