**It** **has been an action-packed start to 2025, with a number of BSME Games taking place across January and early February.**

Gulf Youth Sport was delighted to be at many of these events providing extensive media coverage as students from across the region came together in good-natured competition.

From the athletics track and field to the football pitches and netball courts, we witnessed some incredible individual and team performances across the different age groups.
