**Last weekend saw the BSME U11 Games take place across three host schools in the UAE, with GYS proud to provide extensive media coverage once again**.

Young athletes from across the Middle East took part in athletics, football, basketball, netball, and swimming events, with some incredible individual and team performances on display over the weekend.

The Yellow Games were held at Safa Community School, the Blue Games were hosted by Royal Grammar School Guildford, and Nord Anglia International School AD held the White Games.
