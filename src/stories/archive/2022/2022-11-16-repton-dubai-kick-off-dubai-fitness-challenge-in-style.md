**Repton School Dubai got the Dubai Fitness Challenge 2022 started in style earlier this month as students and staff from throughout the school took to the Repton Circle for a morning of exercise which included lots of fun games and challenges led by Repton PE staff.**

In the weeks that have followed, Repton have been getting creative throughout the school day with surprise drop-in PE lessons being led by local coaches and sports academies. So far lessons have included athletics, table tennis and field hockey - giving Repton students the opportunity to try their hand at sports which may be a little unfamiliar to some of the students.
