**Gulf youngsters thrived in last week’s U13 & U15 World School Games 2023 in Dubai, with many leaving as world champions.**

Schools from around the Gulf were ready for the international challenge to display their talents on the big stage.

Furthermore, eight schools from around the world participated, including teams right here in the Gulf representing Saudi Arabia and the UAE. The games also consisted of schools making long journeys from England, Russia, and Kazakhstan to be able to compete in the international event.

Each school competed in the entertaining three-day event that consisted of swimming, football, and athletics matchups in the U13 & U15 age categories. Additionally, every team looked to gain points from each sport to contribute to the overall U13 & U15 World School Games table points, respectively. Well done to all participating athletes and schools in last week’s action!
