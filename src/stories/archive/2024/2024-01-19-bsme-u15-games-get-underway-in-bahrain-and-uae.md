The first BSME Games multi-sports events of 2024 are underway this weekend in Bahrain and Abu Dhabi, UAE with students from across the region coming together to compete, learn and meet friends new and old.

With so many schools excited to take part in the U15 Games, the competitions are split across three different schools in two countries this month, with St Christopher's School Bahrain, Cranleigh Abu Dhabi and the British International School of Abu Dhabi on hosting duties.

Yesterday was the first day of sports action in Bahrain, with football taking centre stage at St Christopher's School, while today things got underway in Abu Dhabi with boys volleyball and girls football kicking off this morning.
