The UAE netball community is about to receive a masterclass from the very top of the global game.

ICE Education, in association with the Dubai Affiliated School Sports Association (DASSA), has announced a one-off Netball Masterclass led by Dame Noeline Taurua — Head Coach of the world-renowned New Zealand Silver Ferns. The session takes place on Saturday, 5 December 2026, at Dubai College, and represents one of the most significant coaching development opportunities ever brought to the region.

**What Wins Under Pressure**

Titled "What Wins Under Pressure," the masterclass is built around the psychological and tactical blueprints that Dame Noeline has used to build the most successful netball programme on the planet.

The interactive session explores three core pillars that define elite team performance. Clarity focuses on establishing crystal-clear tactical frameworks under match conditions. Connection looks at how players unite to execute seamless decision-making under intense pressure. Composure addresses the development of emotional control and poise in the moments that matter most.

Beyond the tactical instruction, delegates will have the optional opportunity to take to the court and participate in practical drills — gaining firsthand experience of being coached by a World Cup and Commonwealth Gold-winning legend.

**A Decorated Legacy**

Dame Noeline Taurua stands among the most decorated figures in international sport. Currently in her eighth year as Head Coach of the Silver Ferns, she led New Zealand to a historic Netball World Cup title in 2019 — their first in 16 years — before securing Commonwealth Gold in Glasgow earlier this year.

In the 2020 New Year Honours, she became only the third netballer in history to be appointed a Dame Companion of the New Zealand Order of Merit. As a player, she represented the Silver Ferns at the 1995 World Cup and the 1998 Commonwealth Games — a career that laid the foundation for a coaching legacy that has redefined what New Zealand netball is capable of.

**Registration and DASSA Member Benefits**

The masterclass includes full day catering, official digital resource packs, and direct access to world-class coaching insights across the session.

As part of their annual membership benefits, all DASSA member schools receive an exclusive 10% discount on registration — a significant saving on a session of this calibre.

"To bring a world-champion coach of Dame Noeline's caliber to Dubai is a game-changer for regional netball," noted the event organisers. "This masterclass will directly elevate the quality of coaching across our school leagues, academies, and clubs."

Whether you are a school PE teacher, academy coach, or club director, this is a rare opportunity to learn directly from one of the greatest tactical minds the game has ever produced.

Places are strictly limited. Coaches can view full booking details and secure their delegate pass via ICE Education's Official Masterclass Portal. DASSA member schools should quote their membership when booking to apply the 10% discount.

Do not miss it.
