## **Crowned Kings: Kings' School Dubai Edges Out Arcadia in BSME U11 Yellow Games Epic**

The **BSME U11 Games 2026** Yellow Division delivered a historic textbook example of a multi-sport tug-of-war. After three days of exhausting competition, **Kings' School Dubai** emerged as the absolute **Overall Champions**, holding off an incredibly fierce charge from a surging Arcadia British School squad.

The victory was engineered on the track, in the court, and in the pool. Kings’ School Dubai secured crucial **1st place finishes in Swimming and Boys' Athletics**, alongside a vital **2nd place in Boys’ Basketball**. This elite performance across different mediums gave them the definitive points buffer required to claim the ultimate regional crown.

### **Arcadia and Safa Light Up the Arenas**

  
**Arcadia British School** was arguably the most explosive team of the weekend, securing **2nd place overall**. They completely dominated the court and pitch, taking **1st place in Boys' Football, Girls' Netball, and Girls' Athletics**. The athletics track belonged to Arcadia's dynamic duo **Mia and Micah**, who swept the individual Player of the Tournament awards.

Claiming a phenomenal **3rd place overall** was **Safa Community School (SCS)**. The "SCS Sharks" secured a historic **1st place finish in Boys' Basketball**, led by an MVP performance from **Harold** in the swimming pool and **Anaya** on the football pitch.

### **International Flavour**

  
The Yellow Division featured spectacular cross-border battles. Bahrain’s **St. Christopher’s School** took an historic **1st place finish in Girls’ Football**, proving their elite pedigree on the pitch. Qatar's **Doha College** and Dubai’s **DESS Oud Metha** pushed the top tier continuously, with DESS claiming twin MVP accolades in the pool via **Layla and Kenza**.
