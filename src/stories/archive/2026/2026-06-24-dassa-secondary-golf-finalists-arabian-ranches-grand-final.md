## **Elite Field Confirmed: Dubai’s Finest Student Golfers Set for Dramatic Grand Final at Arabian Ranches**

After six grueling, high-pressure qualifying rounds across the emirate’s premier courses, the stage is officially set for the ultimate crowning moment in UAE school golf.

The finalists for the **DASSA Secondary Golf Championship** have been locked in, bringing together a powerhouse field of young swing kings and queens. On **Monday, June 22, 2026**, these elite student-athletes descended upon the pristine but punishing fairways of the **Arabian Ranches Golf Club** for a 2:00 PM shotgun shootout to decide who takes home the ultimate Gross and Net titles.

## **🏫 THE SCHOOL POWERHOUSE BATTLE: REVEALING THE GRID**

  
The qualifying campaign has revealed a fascinating narrative: a heavy-hitting turf war between **GEMS FirstPoint School** and **Brighton College**, with the heavyweights of **Dubai College** and **Safa Community School** lurking dangerously in the shadows.  

### **The GEMS FirstPoint Armada**

  
No school displayed deeper competitive squads this year than GEMS FirstPoint, sending a massive contingent to the finals. The frontline is led by the dangerous **Victor**, alongside elite ball-strikers **Lucia**, **Sven**, **Gustaf**, **Airi**, **Michael**, and **Oscar**. FirstPoint has its sights set on an absolute clean sweep of the podiums.  

### **The Brighton College Surge**

  
Brighton College counter-punches with an incredibly potent lineup of their own. **Bobby**, **Wilf**, and **Ricci** have routinely torn up qualifiers, while the depth of **Jameel**, **Arthur**, **Antonio**, and **Samaira** makes Brighton a massive threat in both the low-Gross and handicap-Net fields.  

### **The Independent Contenders**

  

  
-   **Dubai College:** Bolstered by a highly technical trio featuring **Keiran**, **Alex**, and **Lifu**—all capable of going low under tournament conditions.
  
-   **Safa Community School:** A balanced team consisting of **Angad**, **Sam**, **Freddie**, **Bente**, and **Archie**, who have shown the consistency needed to handle the pressure cooker of Arabian Ranches.
  
-   **Nord Anglia School:** Spearheaded by the strategic trio of **Phillip**, **Lawrie**, and **Shahyar**.
