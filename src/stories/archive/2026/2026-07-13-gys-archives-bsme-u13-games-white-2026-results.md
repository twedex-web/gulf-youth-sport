## **Capital Class: Nord Anglia International School Abu Dhabi Claims Ultimate BSME U13 White Championship**

The **BSME U13 Games White** division delivered an unforgettable weekend of international school sport as 11 premier academies from across the region collided. When the final points were tallied, it was the ruthless consistency of **Nord Anglia International School Abu Dhabi (NAS Abu Dhabi)** that earned the crown, securing **1st place overall** to write their name into the history books.

NAS Abu Dhabi built their championship campaign on clinical execution across multiple sports. They secured runner-up spots in **Girls' Athletics, Girls' Netball, and Boys' Football**, while adding a vital 3rd place finish in **Girls' Football**. This continuous scoring across the court, pitch, and track gave them the definitive point buffer needed to stay clear of a hungry chasing pack.

### **Kuwait and Amity Push the Limit**

  
The race for the overall podium went down to the absolute wire. **The English School (TES) Kuwait** put together an exceptional cross-border campaign to claim **2nd place overall**. The Kuwait-based powerhouse showed immense court discipline, storming the sports hall to take **1st place in Boys' Volleyball**.

Finishing a razor-thin margin behind them in **3rd place overall** was **Amity International School Abu Dhabi**. Amity established themselves as the undisputed kings of the track and field. Their athletes put on a dominant show of power, speed, and endurance to execute a clean sweep, taking **1st place in both Girls' and Boys' Athletics**.

### **Double Delight for Brighton College Dubai**

  
While the capital schools dominated the overall standings, **Brighton College Dubai** proved to be the most lethal tournament bracket team of the weekend. The Dubai school pulled off a spectacular double championship by winning **1st place in both Girls' Netball and Boys' Football**, showcasing incredible tactical maturity under pressure in both finals.

The international flair was further elevated by Egypt's **Maadi British International School**, who turned on a clinic of attacking football to capture **1st place in Girls' Football**.

## **BSME U13 White Division Official Ledger**

  

  
-   **Overall Champions:** Nord Anglia International School Abu Dhabi, UAE 🏆
  
-   **Girls’ Football Winners:** Maadi British International School, Egypt
  
-   **Boys’ Volleyball Winners:** The English School, Kuwait
  
-   **Girls’ Athletics Winners:** Amity International School Abu Dhabi, UAE
  
-   **Boys’ Athletics Winners:** Amity International School Abu Dhabi, UAE
  
-   **Girls’ Netball Winners:** Brighton College Dubai, UAE
  
-   **Boys’ Football Winners:** Brighton College Dubai, UAE
  
-   **Fairplay Award:** British International School Riyadh - DQ, KSA 🤝
