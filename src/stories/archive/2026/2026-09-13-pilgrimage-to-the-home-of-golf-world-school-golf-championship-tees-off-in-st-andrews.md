**Nearly 100 Participants Assembled as Global Field and Middle East Powerhouses Tackle Ladybank, Kittocks, and Torrance**

The long-anticipated **World School Golf Championship** officially gets underway today in Fife, Scotland, bringing nearly 100 junior athletes, senior contenders, and staff delegates together for a week of premier competition across three of the region’s finest courses: **Ladybank**, **Kittocks**, and **The Torrance** at Fairmont St Andrews.

Organized under the World School Games banner, the championship showcases a striking international footprint, uniting school teams and independent entries from across the globe while featuring heavy representation from top Middle East institutions.

### **Gulf Powerhouses Lead the Charge**

  
Leading the regional contingent in Scotland are two of Dubai’s heavyweight sporting schools: **Dubai English Speaking College (DESSC)** and **Brighton College Dubai**, alongside **American International School Riyadh (AIS Riyadh)** representing Saudi Arabia.  

  
-   **DESSC:** Heading into the championship with a formidable nine-player roster including Senior contenders _Freddie Dalton_, _Michael Swart_, _Abdulla Darwish_, _Morgan Barr_, _Robbie Mungin_, _Freddie Barbara_, _Zachary Nye_, and _Shantanu Narayan_, backed by Junior standout _Alexander Grieve_.
  
-   **Brighton College Dubai:** Deploying a deep six-player squad featuring Seniors _Oscar Bell_ and _Riccardo Bianchi_, alongside Junior talents _William Clark_, _Harrison Daniels_, _Jack O'Brien_, and _George Wylie_.
  
-   **AIS Riyadh:** Bringing strong regional representation from the Kingdom, led by Seniors _Khalid Almodaimeegh_, _Gregoire Dhaeyer_, and _Laura Chambers_, alongside Juniors _Dominique Fanderl_, _Harald Fanderl_, and _Sullivan Honig_.
