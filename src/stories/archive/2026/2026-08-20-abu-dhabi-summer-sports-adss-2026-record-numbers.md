**Beat the Heat: Abu Dhabi Summer Sports 2026 Reaches Record Participation Numbers**

The summer months no longer mean a pause in youth athletic development in the UAE. The 2026 edition of **Abu Dhabi Summer Sports (ADSS)**—running from **June 6th through August 23rd** at the **ADNEC Centre Abu Dhabi**—has transformed over 34,000 square meters of air-conditioned space into the region’s premier indoor sports haven.

Providing world-class indoor football pitches, padel courts, basketball hardcourts, and running tracks, ADSS 2026 has become a crucial summer hub for young athletes from leading **ADISSA** and **BSME** schools across the capital, including **Brighton College Abu Dhabi**, **Cranleigh Abu Dhabi**, and **NAS Abu Dhabi**.

### **Maintaining Match Readiness Year-Round**

  
By bridging the gap between academic terms, the climate-controlled megastructure allows youth academies, school squads, and community teams to maintain their technical speed, endurance, and game-tactics during peak summer months.

The initiative aligns directly with the UAE National Sports Strategy, encouraging active lifestyles and providing structured performance platforms 365 days a year.
