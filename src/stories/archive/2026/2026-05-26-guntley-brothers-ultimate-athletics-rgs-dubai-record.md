## **Track Royalty: Cody and Jayden Guntley Dominate the Arena with Sibling Trebles**

Every now and then, a single family completely takes over a racetrack. That was exactly the case this weekend as brothers **Cody and Jayden Guntley** put on an absolute masterclass in sprinting and jumping.

The sibling duo, who study at **Royal Grammar School (RGS) Guildford Dubai** and train with **Ultimate Athletics**, left fields in their wake. Together, they secured a staggering six gold medals across the junior divisions. It was a historic performance that culminated in a spectacular new UAE club record.

### **Cody Guntley: The Under-12 Clean Sweep**

  
At just 10 years old, **Cody Guntley** proved he is one of the most versatile athletes in his age bracket. Competing in the Under-12 category, Cody showcased a lethal mix of explosive power and flat-line speed.

He secured a flawless triple gold medal haul, dominating both the **80m and 150m sprint events** on the track. He then took to the pit to secure his third gold in the **Under-12 Long Jump**. His ability to switch from the technical demands of jumping to pure sprinting marks him as a massive prospect for the future.

### **Jayden Guntley: The Record-Breaker**

  
Not to be outdone by his younger brother, 12-year-old **Jayden Guntley** delivered an equally devastating performance in the Under-14 division. Jayden mirrored Cody’s treble by capturing gold in the **Under-14 Long Jump and the 300m**.

However, his crowning achievement came in the **Under-14 150m**. Blasting out of the blocks, Jayden stopped the clock at an incredible **18.96 seconds**. This time officially sets a brand-new **UAE Academy Club Record**, making him the fastest U14 athlete across all academies in the Emirates.

### **The Ultimate Blueprint**

  
The brothers' historic weekend is a massive testament to the synergy between school and club sports. The foundational support from **RGS Guildford Dubai**, combined with the elite-level coaching at **Ultimate Athletics**, has created the perfect launchpad for the Guntley brothers.

With six gold medals and a UAE record now safely in the family trophy cabinet, the Guntley name is officially written into the regional athletics folklore.

## **The Guntley Medal Haul**

  

  
-   **Cody Guntley (U12):** 🥇 80m Gold | 🥇 150m Gold | 🥇 Long Jump Gold
  
-   **Jayden Guntley (U14):** 🥇 150m Gold _(UAE Club Record: 18.96s)_ | 🥇 300m Gold | 🥇 Long Jump Gold
