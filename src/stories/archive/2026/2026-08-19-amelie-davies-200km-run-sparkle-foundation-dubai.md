## **Running for Hope: Youth Athlete Amelie Davies Tackles 200+ km September Challenge for The Sparkle Foundation**

At Gulf Youth Sport, we love seeing young athletes use their athletic passion to change lives.

This September, talented Dubai youth runner **Amelie Davies** is stepping onto the pavement to take on her most ambitious challenge yet: clocking an astonishing **200+ kilometers** throughout the month to raise vital funds and global awareness for **The Sparkle Foundation**.

### **From Grassroots Passion to Podium Finishes**

  
Amelie’s athletic journey began three years ago when she joined **Ultimate Athletics Club** in Dubai. What started as a fun grass-roots hobby quickly developed into an unwavering passion.

Over the past few seasons, Amelie has grown into one of the UAE running circuit's most consistent performers, securing multiple podium finishes and building an appetite for longer distances. Training alongside supportive communities including Ultimate Athletics and New Auto Activity, Amelie credits her fellow runners for constantly inspiring her growth.

_"I started running 3 years ago and since then, it has become one of the biggest parts of my life,"_ says Amelie. _"I love challenging myself and competing on the UAE circuit, but for me, running has been about so much more than races and medals."_

### **Running for a Cause: The Sparkle Foundation**

  
Having previously used her long-distance running to support Macmillan Cancer Support UK and local initiatives, Amelie is now directing her energy toward **The Sparkle Foundation**.

The internationally recognized charity works directly with communities in Malawi to provide:

  
-   📚 **Education:** Opening doors for underprivileged children to build bright futures.
  
-   🏥 **Healthcare:** Delivering essential medical support to families in need.
  
-   🍎 **Nutrition:** Ensuring children have the daily fuel needed to grow and learn.
  
-   🤝 **Community Empowerment:** Creating self-sufficient, sustainable community ecosystems.
  

  
Through her 200+ km pledge this September, Amelie hopes to prove that age is never a barrier when it comes to giving back.

_"I hope that by doing something I love, I can make a difference, inspire other young people, and show that you are never too young to help others,"_ Amelie shares.

## **🎯 AMELIE'S SEPTEMBER MISSION**

  

  
-   🏃‍♀️ **The Target:** Complete **200+ kilometers** across September.
  
-   💡 **The Vision:** Raise vital funds and international awareness for The Sparkle Foundation.
  
-   ✨ **The Goal:** Inspire young athletes to lead healthy, active lifestyles while supporting global communities.
  

  
Everyone at Gulf Youth Sport is firmly in Amelie’s corner as she prepares to start logging her miles!
