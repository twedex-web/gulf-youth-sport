**Global Dominance, Local Heritage: JESS Dubai and Dubai College Lead Gulf Charge at the World Schools Netball Cup in Loughborough**

The world’s most prestigious showcase of youth netball has once again delivered a masterclass in high-stakes competition. The sixth edition of the **World Schools Netball Cup**, proudly backed by headline sponsor **Twedex**, took over the world-renowned sports facilities at **Loughborough University** last month.

With **Unite Teamwear** serving as the official merchandise provider, the event took on a truly global flavor. Over 350 elite players representing 26 schools from seven countries and four continents converged on the UK. Under the bright lights of the Sir David Wallace and Netball Centres—home to Netball Super League heavyweights Loughborough Lightning—the weekend was defined by high-intensity tactical battles and a massive step forward for Middle Eastern netball programs.

### **Middle East Powerhouses Trade Blows with the Best**

  
While the tournament welcomed powerhouse newcomers from as far as Australia and Namibia, it was the UAE contingent that repeatedly forced their way into the elite global spotlight.

The **U13 Division** was a spectacular showcase of Gulf excellence. **JESS Dubai** put on a masterclass of clinical passing and defensive coordination throughout the tournament, storming all the way to the Grand Cup Final. In a classic, nail-biting encounter against England’s Hulme Grammar School, JESS jumped to a commanding early lead before ultimately taking home a heroic global **Silver Medal**.

Right behind the elite tier contenders, **Dubai College** showcased their exceptional squad depth and resilience to triumphantly lift the **U13 Plate Championship** trophy.

In the **U15 Division**, **JESS Dubai** proved their consistency on the international stage once more. Displaying outstanding transition play and clinical shooting under immense defensive pressure, the Dubai-based school fought off top international academy fields to capture a thoroughly deserved global **Bronze Medal**.

### **The Global All-Star Spectacle**

  
Before the main tournament brackets commenced, the tournament celebrated its massive international footprint with the fan-favorite **WSG Netball All-Star Matches**.

In front of packed bleachers, players from across the continents united to represent **Team Rest of the World** against **Team UK** across the U13, U15, and Senior brackets. The exhibition served as a fantastic reminder of the tournament's core ethos: bringing the youth sporting community together at a world-class level.
