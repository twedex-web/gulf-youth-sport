**Grassroots Revolution: Student-Led Origin FC Defies the Odds Through United Squad Leadership**

Every so often, a club emerges that completely redefines what is possible across the region's footballing landscape. Meet Origin FC, a grassroots sensation built on shared leadership and unyielding squad unity that has just put together one of the most remarkable growth stories in Dubai's youth sports history.

Founded by Daniel Riachi, a passionate 16-year-old student who was priced out of the city's steep academy system, Origin FC was created to make competitive football accessible to everyday players. What started as Daniel's vision quickly transformed into a collective mission powered by a dedicated team of student leaders.

**Collective Strength: How the Core Leadership Built the Foundation**

When the Youth Football League (YFL) Dubai gave Origin FC the green light to enter, the club's survival depended entirely on its core leadership stepping up to divide and conquer the off-pitch hurdles:

-   **Founder & Operational Direction:** Daniel Riachi spearheaded the project from scratch, pitching the YFL and assembling the foundational squad while steering the vision.
    
-   **Operations & Financial Balance:** Nirmay Teckchandani stepped in as the operational backbone, managing the finances, structuring fair fee splits, pitching local sponsors to keep player costs to an absolute minimum, and overseeing the overall structure and format of the club.
    
-   **Purpose & Community Impact:** President of Partnerships Shivank Mishra anchored the squad's values by securing an official partnership with Muskaan PAEPID (a charity supporting mentally differently-abled individuals), putting their name on the match kits to raise awareness.
    
-   **Culture & Resilience:** Chief Operating Officer Alexander Riachi served as the glue for the entire roster, holding team morale together off the pitch after a brutal 8-1 opening-day defeat and maintaining unity through an early losing streak.
    
-   **Coaching & On-Pitch Growth:** Head Coach Mazen Kais provided the technical structure and confidence the squad lacked, orchestrating a massive turnaround that culminated in a 7-game winning streak and a 3rd-place league finish.
    

**Building the Pipeline: Expanding the Origin FC Family**

Refusing to stop with a single squad, Head of Football Operations Ruben Hunt took on the challenge of building the club's next generation. Single-handedly developing a brand-new U14 squad from scratch, he guided them through a demanding summer league against older opposition to establish a sustainable path for younger players.

Now, heading into the 2026/2027 season, Alexander Riachi’s crucial negotiations behind the scenes have officially elevated Origin FC into the prestigious DOFA league. The original U17 core will step up into the U19 DOFA division, while Ruben’s U14 squad continues its journey in the YFL—proving that when a team shares the load, ordinary players can build extraordinary pathways.

**Origin FC: The Team & Growth Breakdown**

**The Leadership Structure**

-   **Daniel Riachi (Founder & Vision):** Spearheaded the league entry, assembled the initial squad, and set the core mission of affordable football
    
-   **Nirmay Teckchandani (Operations):** Managed club structure, operations, finances, budget allocations, and sponsor acquisition
    
-   **Shivank Mishra (Partnerships):** Secured the Muskaan PAEPID charity kit alignment
    
-   **Alexander Riachi (COO):** Maintained squad cohesion, off-field operations, and DOFA negotiations
    
-   **Mazen Kais (Head Coach):** Tactical lead behind the 7-game winning streak and 3rd-place finish
    
-   **Ruben Hunt (Football Operations):** Built and managed the youth U14 development squad
    

**2026/2027 Team Placements**

-   **U19 Division:** Promoted to the DOFA League
    
-   **U14 Division:** Full season placement in the YFL
