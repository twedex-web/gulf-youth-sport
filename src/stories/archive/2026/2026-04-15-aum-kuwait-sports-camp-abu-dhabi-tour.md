## The American University of the Middle East (AUM) swaps Kuwait for Abu Dhabi in a sports training camp with TWEDEX.

In October 2025, the **[American University of the Middle East (AUM)](https://www.aum.edu.kw/)** took their pre-season preparations to a professional level, touching down in Abu Dhabi for a multi-sport tour that set the tone for their upcoming competitive season.

Facilitated by the sports travel experts at **[TWEDEX](https://twedex.com/)**, the tour brought together athletes from AUM’s Men’s and Women’s football, basketball, and padel squads for a weekend of elite-level training and cross-border competition.

### AUM Kuwait Sports Camp

The action kicked off at the world-class **[321 Sports](https://321sports.ae/)** on Hudayriyat Island, a facility known for its FIFA-standard pitches and the largest indoor sports dome in the MENA region.

The Men's football side delivered a standout performance, securing a hard-fought victory against local opposition **Ole FC**. Meanwhile, the Women’s football and basketball squads faced off against **[Abu Dhabi University (ADU)](https://www.adu.ac.ae/)** in a series of highly competitive fixtures that served as the perfect litmus test before their regular seasons.

The Padel athletes were equally active. They headed to the **Gaby Reca Centre** to compete against some of the UAE’s top club players. Beyond the results, the trip focused on recovery and team culture. This included a professional recovery session at the hotel and a "retail therapy" visit to **Yas Mall** before the short hop back to Kuwait.

### **Word from the Athletes & Staff**

  

The success of the tour was echoed by the AUM leadership. **Liam O’Brien**, Athletic Director at AUM, praised the seamless organization:

> _"Another exceptional trip with Twedex... The team went above and beyond to ensure the athletes had everything they needed to perform at their best. Professionalism at its finest."_

The students were equally vocal about the experience. One student athlete remarked, _"Impeccably organized, making for a memorable and enjoyable experience for all involved,"_ while another added, _"It was a pretty incredible trip that surpassed our expectations. The challenge to step out of our comfort zone was exactly what we needed."_

### **The GYS Take: Looking Toward the Gulf University Games**

As GYS expands its coverage into the university sector, seeing powerhouse institutions like AUM Kuwait investing in high-performance tours highlights the rising standard of university athletics in the region.

By prioritizing world-class facilities and high-performance coaching, AUM is setting a new standard for the university sports pathway in the Gulf. This is an evolution similar to the rise of **[digital scouting platforms like Kick Connect](/2026/04/07/the-future-of-scouting/)**, which are helping local talents bridge the gap between school athletics and global opportunities.
