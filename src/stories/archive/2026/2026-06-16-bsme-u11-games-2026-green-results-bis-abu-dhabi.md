## **Capital Supremacy: The British International School Abu Dhabi Sweeps BSME U11 Green Games Title**

The **BSME U11 Games 2026** Green Division crown is heading to the capital. In a spectacular showcase of junior athletic depth, **The British International School Abu Dhabi (BISAD)** put on an absolute masterclass in all-round consistency, comfortably securing the **Overall Championship** title against 13 elite regional academies.

BISAD's victory was a testament to their balanced athletic pipeline. The Abu Dhabi powerhouse secured top finishes in four out of six core sports disciplines. They claimed **1st place in Boys’ Basketball, Girls’ Athletics, Boys’ Athletics, and Swimming**. It was a relentless accumulation of points that left the rest of the field chasing silver.

### **The Chasers: Kent College and Brighton Secure Podiums**

  
The battle for the remaining podium steps was an entirely Dubai-centric affair. **Kent College Dubai** put together a fantastic campaign to claim **2nd place overall**. The school dominated the court and pitch, taking **1st place in both Girls' Football and Girls' Netball**, driven by an MVP performance from **Lucia** in the netball ring.

Securing a hard-earned **3rd place overall** was **Brighton College Dubai**. They relied heavily on their aquatic strength to edge out Ranches Primary School, picking up a massive **2nd place finish in Swimming**.

### **Individual Excellence**

  
While BISAD owned the team leaderboards, individual star power illuminated the division. BISAD's track stars swept the individual honours, with **Isabel** named Girls' Athletics Player of the Tournament and **Teddy** matching her feat for the boys.

On the football pitches, **Horizon International School** shook up the establishment to win **1st place in Boys' Football**, anchored by tournament MVP **Omar** from Safa British School. Meanwhile, **Amani** of The English School Kuwait took home the Girls' Football MVP accolade.
