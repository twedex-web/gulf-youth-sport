## **GYS Flashback: British International School Cairo Crowned Champions in Epic BSME U15 Games Blue**

In this edition of the _GYS Archive Vault_, we look back at a fierce multi-sport continental campaign. The **BSME U15 Games Blue (2025)** brought together 10 leading academies from Egypt, the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, and Kuwait for a high-stakes, multi-discipline showdown.

When the points were finalized across football, netball, volleyball, and athletics, Egypt’s **British International School Cairo (BISC)** produced a masterclass in multi-sport consistency to claim the title of **Overall BSME U15 Games Blue Champions**.

[View this post on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DRl-fsAE4q1/)

### **The Overall Podium: BISC Edge Out UAE Powerhouses**

  
BISC’s championship campaign was anchored by a gold medal performance in Girls' Football and top-tier finishes in Boys' Volleyball (2nd) and Boys' Football (3rd).

Trailing closely behind was a formidable UAE contingent. **Brighton College Abu Dhabi** delivered an exceptional tournament run to claim **2nd Place Overall (Silver)**—capped off by a dominant Gold-medal campaign in Boys' Football. Completing the podium in **3rd Place Overall (Bronze)**, **Brighton College Dubai** put on an absolute masterclass in girls' sports, securing a rare double Gold sweep across both Girls' Athletics and Girls' Netball.
