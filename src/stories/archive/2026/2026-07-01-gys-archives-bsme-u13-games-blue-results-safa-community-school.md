## **Ultimate Consistency: Safa Community School Clinches Overall BSME U13 Blue Championship**

The **BSME U13 Games Blue** provided a spectacular celebration of elite school sport as 12 of the Middle East’s premier academies descended upon Dubai. After three days of high-intensity multi-sport drama, **Safa Community School (SCS)** produced a masterclass in all-round athletic depth to claim the coveted **Overall Championship** title.

Safa’s route to the summit was forged through pure dominance on the track and field. The Dubai-based school executed a clean sweep of the athletics programme, securing **1st place in both Girls' and Boys' Athletics**. This massive points haul, paired with strong, consistent top-five brackets across the court and pitch disciplines, gave Safa the definitive edge in a fiercely contested leaderboard.

### **The Chasers: Horizon and Sunmarke Secure Podiums**

  
The battle for the remaining overall podium places went down to the final rotation of fixtures. **Horizon International School** put together a stunning, well-rounded campaign to secure **2nd place overall**. Horizon proved to be the masters of the court, capturing **1st place in Boys' Volleyball** and packing out top-four finishes in multiple sports.

Securing a hard-earned **3rd place overall** was **Sunmarke School**. Sunmarke relied heavily on their attacking prowess and depth, picking up a vital **2nd place in Boys’ Athletics** and a **3rd place finish in Boys’ Football** to keep their podium ambitions alive until the final medal ceremony.

### **Pitch and Ring Dominance**

  
While Safa owned the overall leaderboard, individual team sports saw other powerhouses split the silverware. **Dubai British School Jumeirah Park (DBS JP)** proved completely unstoppable in female team sports, executing a flawless double by winning **1st place in both Girls' Football and Girls' Netball**.

On the boys' side, **Yasmina British Academy** hit peak form during the knockout stages of the football tournament, playing lightning-fast transitional football to capture **1st place in Boys' Football**.
