## **Azzurri to the Desert: Former Italian Rugby International Aaron Persico Launches Elite Rugby Academy in Dubai**

The UAE’s rugby landscape has officially gained a massive injection of Test-match pedigree. Former Italian international flanker **Aaron Persico** has announced the official launch of the **Dubai Broncos**—a premier youth rugby training program designed to bring elite, modern-era technical development to the next generation of Gulf-based players.

Persico, a ferocious back-rower who accumulated **56 caps for Italy** and represented the Azzurri at the **2003 Rugby World Cup**, is one of the most recognizable faces of Italian rugby's foundational Six Nations era. Now, he is deploying his vast European and international high-performance knowledge directly onto the fields of Dubai.

### **A Modern Approach to Grassroots Rugby**

  
Dubai Broncos enters the market with a clear mission: to refine technical skills, build tactical intelligence, and foster the distinct core values of rugby culture. The academy focuses on individual skill tracking, positional precision, and structured spatial awareness.

By combining his professional playing experience across the highest tiers of European club and international rugby with a passion for youth development, Persico aims to create a world-class development pathway that bridges the gap between school-level rugby and international age-group standards.

"Our goal is to give young players in the UAE the absolute best platform to develop their skills and character," says the academy coaching staff. "Rugby is about discipline, teamwork, and resilience. We are bringing a professional environment where athletes can learn the technical nuances of the game directly from those who have played it at the absolute absolute highest level."
