## **Set for Greatness: WSG Announces the Inaugural World School Volleyball Championships in Antalya**

**Location:** Gloria Sports Arena, Antalya, Turkey **Dates:** 27th – 30th November 2026 **Age Groups:** U16 & U19 (Boys & Girls)

The World School Games Event Series has officially expanded its global footprint with the launch of its eighth signature event: **The World School Volleyball Championships**. Following a highly successful pilot event in November 2025, the series is heading back to the "Capital of Turkish Tourism"—Antalya—for the first full-scale edition of the tournament.

This isn’t just another school tournament; it is a professional-grade experience designed to give the stars of tomorrow a platform to compete on the world stage.

### **The Venue: An Olympic-Standard Stage**

  
The championships will be hosted at the prestigious **Gloria Sports Arena**, the largest sports facility in Turkey and an official Olympic Training Centre.  

  
-   **Live Like Pros:** The facility features an on-site athlete hotel where teams will live, train, and compete in a professional "bubble."
  
-   **Beyond the Court:** When not competing on the state-of-the-art show courts, students will have access to world-class gyms, the famous 'Icelab,' swimming pools, and recovery saunas.

### **Tournament Format**

The four-day event will welcome **32 teams** from across the globe.

-   **Categories:** U16 Boys, U16 Girls, U19 Boys, and U19 Girls.
-   **The Journey:** Group stage matches followed by tiered knockout rounds, ensuring every school competes for silverware, from the main Cup to the Plate and Shield.

_"Giving young people the chance to perform in top-class environments is a key feature of our events,"_ said **Daniel Shields**, Head of the World School Games Event Series. _"We can't wait to see the world's best school volleyball programs descend on Antalya."_

**Be Part of History:** Spaces are strictly limited to 32 teams on a first-come, first-served basis.
