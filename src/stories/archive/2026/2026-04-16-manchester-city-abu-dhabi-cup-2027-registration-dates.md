### **Manchester City Abu Dhabi Cup 2027: Global football phenomenon returns to the capital**

Following a record-breaking 2026 edition that saw the UAE capital transformed into a global hub for youth football, the **Manchester City Abu Dhabi Cup** has officially confirmed its return for 2027.

Scheduled to take place from **January 8 to 10, 2027**, the eighth edition of the tournament is set to build on a legacy of elite competition, cultural exchange, and world-class sporting hospitality at **Zayed Sports City**.

### **![Winning team lifting the trophy at the Manchester City Abu Dhabi Cup.](/images/archive/2026/04/abu-dhabi-cup-5.webp)**

  

### **A Global Stage in the Heart of the UAE**

  
The 2026 tournament redefined the scale of youth football in the Middle East. With over **8,000 spectators** creating an electric atmosphere, the event hosted **3,500 players** from four different continents.

Of the **244 teams** that competed, a staggering **86 traveled from overseas**, representing 18 different countries. From local powerhouses to elite international academies, the Manchester City Abu Dhabi Cup has cemented its reputation as the premier platform for the next generation of footballing talent.

### **The Rise of the Girls’ Game**

  
One of the standout success stories from the recent edition was the unprecedented growth of the female categories. A record-breaking **44 girls' teams** took to the pitch in 2026, showcasing the rapid development of women’s football in the region—a trend the 2027 edition aims to accelerate even further. This follows the trend of excellence seen in the **DASSA Padel Championships** where female participation is at an all-time high.  

### **The "City" Experience**

  
The three-day format, which has proven immensely popular with players and parents alike, offers more than just trophies. It provides a professional-grade experience, from the pristine pitches of Zayed Sports City to the "City Village" fan zones.

For many traveling teams, the tournament serves as the centerpiece of a wider [**TWEDEX**](http://www.twedex.com/) sports tour, blending high-stakes football with the iconic landmarks of Abu Dhabi.

### **SECURE YOUR SPOT FOR 2027**

  
With global interest at an all-time high and spots filling faster than ever, early registration is highly recommended for teams looking to test themselves on this prestigious stage.  

  
-   **Dates:** January 8 – 10, 2027
  
-   **Location:** Zayed Sports City, Abu Dhabi
  
-   **Age Groups:** Under-8 to Under-16 (Boys & Girls categories)
