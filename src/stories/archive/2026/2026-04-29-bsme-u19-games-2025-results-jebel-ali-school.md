The pinnacle of the British Schools in the Middle East (BSME) sporting calendar took center stage in November 2025, as 20 premier institutions descended upon Dubai for the BSME U19 Games. Hosted at the world-class facilities of **Jebel Ali School (JAS)** and meticulously organized by **TWEDEX**, the weekend was a showcase of the highest level of youth athletics in the region.

From the first whistle to the final trophy presentation, the tournament brought together hundreds of elite athletes, testing their mettle across Football, Netball, and Volleyball in a format designed to push the boundaries of school-level competition.

### **The Battle for the Podium**

  
After three days of intense rivalry, **Cranleigh Abu Dhabi** emerged as the **Overall 2025 Champions**. Their dominance was underpinned by clinical performances, specifically taking home the gold in both **Girls’ Football** and **Girls’ Netball**.

The overall podium was a tight race:

  
-   🏆 **1st Place:** Cranleigh Abu Dhabi (UAE)
  
-   🥈 **2nd Place:** St. Christopher’s School (Bahrain)
  
-   🥉 **3rd Place:** The British International School Abu Dhabi (BISAD)
  

  
Special mention must go to **British International School Cairo (BISC)**, who showcased the international reach of the games by securing 1st place in the **Boys’ Football**, while **BIS Abu Dhabi** dominated the **Boys' Volleyball** court to take home the top prize.

### **Individual Brilliance: The MVPs**

  
While the team results were the talk of the tournament, the **Player of the Tournament** awards highlighted the future stars of the game:  

  
-   **Girls’ Football:** Sophie (Cranleigh Abu Dhabi)
  
-   **Boys’ Volleyball:** Kristof (The British International School of Abu Dhabi)
  
-   **Girls’ Netball:** Sienna (Kings' School Al Barsha)
  
-   **Boys’ Football:** Robert (Brighton College Dubai)
  

  

### **A Masterclass in Event Management**

  
Hosting an event of this magnitude—featuring schools from the UAE, Bahrain, Egypt, Qatar, KSA, Kuwait, and Oman—requires precision logistics. TWEDEX, acting as the official event partner, ensured that every detail—from official scoring to athlete hospitality—was handled with professional-grade care.

The partnership between Jebel Ali School and TWEDEX provided the perfect stage for these young athletes to shine, proving once again why Dubai remains the global destination for international school sport.
