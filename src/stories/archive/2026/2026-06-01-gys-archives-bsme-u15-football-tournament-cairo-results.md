## **Continental Conquest: JESS Dubai Claims Boys’ BSME U15 Title in High-Stakes Cairo Finals**

The **BSME U15 Football Tournament** delivered a phenomenal weekend of elite youth football as the premier school programs across the Middle East converged on Egypt. Navigating a challenging international itinerary managed seamlessly by global travel experts **TWEDEX**, Dubai’s football powerhouses proved their elite pedigree on foreign soil during an intense three days of pitch battle.

When the dust settled in Cairo, the headline story belonged to **Jumeirah English Speaking School (JESS) Dubai**. The UAE school put on an absolute masterclass of high-pressing, tactical football to capture the **1st place championship crown in the Boys' Tournament**, defeating a lethal home-soil contingent to hoist the continental trophy.

### **JESS and DESC Star on the International Stage**

  
With **TWEDEX** handling the complex tournament logistics, the traveling UAE squads could focus purely on athletic performance. JESS Dubai put on a clinic in the boys' division. They controlled possession from the group stages through to the final, holding off local heavyweights **El Alsson British International School** to secure gold.

**Dubai English Speaking College (DESC)** ensured the UAE occupied multiple steps on the boys' podium. The Dubai team battled through an unforgiving knockout bracket with defensive discipline, locking down a fantastic **3rd place finish in the world-class field**.

### **Egyptian Excellence In the Girls' Bracket**

  
While Dubai ruled the boys' side, the home teams dominated the female division. **El Alsson British International School** turned the girls' tournament into a display of pure dominance. Their clinical finishing and sharp passing sequences earned them **1st place**, holding off a spirited, energetic run from runners-up **Uppingham Cairo**.

The JESS Dubai girls' squad ensured their travel bags returned heavy, matching their male counterparts' podium presence. The team showcased spectacular transitional football to claim **3rd place overall**, cementing JESS as one of the most balanced football setups in the entire region.
