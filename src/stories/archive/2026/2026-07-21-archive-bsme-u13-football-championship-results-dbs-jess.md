## **Headline: GYS Flashback: Remembering Doha British School’s Historic Double Trophy Landslide at the BSME Football Championship**

In this edition of the _GYS Archive Vault_, we look back at one of the most dominant continental campaigns ever produced by a Gulf international school. Late last year, the region's elite descended upon Cairo, Egypt, for the prestigious **BSME (British Schools in the Middle East) U13 Football Tournament**—an event that would ultimately belong to **Doha British School (DBS), Qatar**.

The tournament pitted the finest British international schools from across the Middle East against one another in a blistering, high-stakes battle for regional supremacy. When the dust settled on the Cairo pitches, the DBS Lions didn't just compete—they entirely conquered, securing a jaw-dropping double trophy haul across the Boys' and Girls' competitive divisions.

## **🏆 THE CUP CHAMPIONS: DBS U13 GIRLS TAKE ELITE GOLD**

  
The DBS U13 Girls squad put on an absolute masterclass in tournament football, fighting their way to the ultimate summit of the weekend: **The Cup Competition**.

Navigating the elite tier of the bracket, the girls displayed incredible technical prowess, structural discipline, and tactical brilliance. In a high-pressure finale, they defeated Egypt’s own powerhouse, **El Alsson International School**, to officially hoist the Cup and claim the undisputed crown as the top U13 girls' team in the Middle East.

## **🛡️ THE SHIELD CHAMPIONS: DBS U13 BOYS DOMINATE**

  
Not to be outdone, the DBS U13 Boys squad produced an equally epic tournament run to secure silverware of their own. Competing in the fiercely contested **Shield Competition**, the boys played with relentless heart, clinical attacking transitions, and unmatched determination.

They capped off their tournament by overwhelming **Repton Cairo** in the finals to secure 1st place and take home the official BSME Shield championship trophy back to Doha.

## **🇦🇪 UAE GLORY: JESS DUBAI CAPTURES THE BOYS' CUP**

  
While Qatar ruled the girls' bracket, the United Arab Emirates staked its claim on the boys' elite division. **Jumeirah English Speaking School (JESS), UAE**, put on an absolute clinic to capture 1st place in the **Boys’ Cup Competition**.

JESS fought off stiff continental competition, ultimately outlasting Egypt's **El Alsson International School** in a thriller to ensure a UAE school sat on top of the ultimate boys' podium.
