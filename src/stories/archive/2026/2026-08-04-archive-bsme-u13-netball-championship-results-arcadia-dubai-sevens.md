**GYS Flashback: Arcadia British School Claims Glory at BSME U13 Netball Championship**

In this edition of the _GYS Archive Vault_, we look back at a defining chapter in Gulf youth netball history. Staged at the iconic **The Sevens Stadium** in Dubai and hosted by **Jumeirah College**, the **BSME (British Schools in the Middle East) U13 Netball Championship** brought together 24 elite school programs from across the region for an unforgettable showcase of tactical speed, clinical shooting, and relentless court coverage.

When the dust settled after days of high-octane competition, it was **Arcadia British School** that etched their name into the history books, navigating a grueling knockout bracket to be crowned undisputed BSME Champions.

## **🏆 THE CUP COMPETITION: ARCADIA’S HISTORIC CROWN**

  
The elite Cup tier delivered nail-biting drama from the opening center pass. **Arcadia British School** put on an absolute masterclass in tournament play, combining impenetrable circle defense with ruthless transition speed to march all the way to the top of the podium and claim 1st place.

In a fiercely contested top-four field, traditional powerhouses **Dubai English Speaking College (DESC)** pushed Arcadia to the absolute limit to secure a remarkable 2nd place Silver finish, while **Brighton College Dubai** put together a stunning tournament run to earn a well-deserved 3rd place Bronze. **The British School Al Khubairat (BSAK)** rounded out an elite top four in a tournament that showcased the incredible depth of UAE netball.

## **🛡️ THE PLATE & BOWL CHAMPIONS**

  
The drama extended deep into the secondary brackets, where two more UAE powerhouses demonstrated exceptional tactical depth to lift silverware of their own.  

  
-   🛡️ **The Plate Championship:** Capitalizing on incredible court width and fast-break precision, **Cranleigh Abu Dhabi** dominated the Plate tier to take home 1st place, narrowly holding off a hard-charging **Dubai College** squad (2nd) and **Nord Anglia International School Dubai** (3rd).
  
-   🥣 **The Bowl Championship:** In the Bowl tier, **Regent International School** put together a clinical run of performances to secure 1st place, edging out **The English College Dubai** (2nd) and **South View School** (3rd).
