**UAE Student Nirmay Teckchandani Launches 'Kick Connect' to Bridge the Global Scouting Gap**

In an era where digital platforms are redefining how talent is discovered, 17-year-old Dubai student **Nirmay Teckchandani** is making a massive play in the world of football. With the launch of **Kick Connect**, Nirmay has created a dedicated bridge between aspiring young ballers and the global network of professional coaches and scouts.

The philosophy behind Kick Connect is as simple as it is powerful: talent is universal, but opportunity is not. For too long, elite scouting has relied on traditional, often closed networks, leaving countless grassroots players without a clear path to the professional game. Nirmay’s solution puts the power of exposure directly back into the hands of the players.

**![](/images/archive/2026/04/kickconnect-banner-1.webp)A Digital Showcase for the Next Generation**

Kick Connect functions as a high-performance digital portfolio. Players can upload match highlights, track their technical stats, and build a comprehensive footballing profile accessible to professionals worldwide. By stripping away geographical and financial barriers, the platform creates a merit-based "level playing field"—essential for talent in regions where traditional scouting can be limited.

What truly sets Kick Connect apart is its focus on direct, authentic connection. By bypassing traditional intermediaries, players can present their true selves to scouts who are constantly hunting for a broader, more diverse talent pool.

**Innovation in the UAE**

Nirmay’s initiative is a prime example of the youth-driven innovation currently surging through the UAE. It proves that a deep passion for the "beautiful game," combined with an entrepreneurial mindset, can solve real-world sporting challenges. As the football world continues to globalize, Kick Connect is poised to become a vital tool for any player dreaming of a professional career.

**A Journey Supported by Mentorship**

Success is rarely a solo sprint, and Nirmay is quick to credit the community that helped bring his vision to life. He extends a massive thank you to his **Director of Sport at DIA Emirates Hills, Mihail Kouzev**, for acting as an invaluable mentor. Additionally, Nirmay expresses his sincere gratitude to **Calum Erskine**, whose guidance was instrumental in helping him gain the essential skills needed to reach this milestone.

Nirmay also expresses deep gratitude to **Navin Valrani**, whose mentorship was instrumental in shaping the strategic vision for Kick Connect. Finally, he shares a heartfelt thank you to his parents, **Neeraj and Jyoti Teckchandani**, for their unwavering support as he navigates the worlds of both elite sport and tech innovation.
