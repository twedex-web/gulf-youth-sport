## **Beyond the Rankings: Why Rafa Aldemita is the undisputed King of Junior Tennis in the Gulf**

In the world of competitive youth tennis, rankings often tell only half the story. For **Alexi Luca Rafael "Rafa" Aldemita**, the data is clear: he has spent the last year simply unchallenged on the local circuit.

The **i.Cademy Middle East** student and **CF Tennis Academy** star dominated the **UAE Tennis Federation (UAETF)** U14 rankings throughout 2025. He held the world-class No. 1 spot consistently until March 2026. Rafa only moved from the top spot because he transitioned away from U14 play—having effectively cleared the field in the Emirates.

### **Dominating the "Road to Mubadala"**

  
Rafa’s 2025 season was defined by his victory at the **Road to Mubadala Abu Dhabi Open Masters**. Taking the U14 Championship at this prestigious event cemented his status as the premier junior in the UAE.

He followed this success by sweeping the **1st Dubai Sports Council Tennis Championships** in June 2025. In a show of pure versatility, Rafa claimed both the **U14 and U16 titles**, proving he could beat older and more physical opponents with tactical precision.

### **Conquering the Asian Stage**

  
Rafa has now shifted his focus to international territory. His rise through the **Asian Tennis Federation (ATF)** rankings has been meteoric. On February 16, 2026, he achieved a career-high **Asian ranking of No. 14 in the U14 category**.

His international trophy cabinet is filling up fast:

  
-   **December 2025 (Dubai):** Double Champion (U14 & U16 Singles) at the ATF International Tournament.
  
-   **December 2025 (Doha):** U14 Doubles Champion at the ATF Tournament in Qatar.
  

  

### **The "Student-Athlete" Blueprint**

  
Success at this level requires a unique environment. By schooling with **i.Cademy Middle East**, Rafa has the flexibility to travel to Doha, Abu Dhabi, and across Asia for elite competition. Supported by **Head** and his coaches at **CF Tennis**, he is now operating on a professional-grade schedule.

Rafa Aldemita is no longer just a "rising star" in Dubai. He is a top-tier contender on the Asian stage. As he continues to test himself against the best in the continent, the "Road to the Pros" looks shorter than ever.
