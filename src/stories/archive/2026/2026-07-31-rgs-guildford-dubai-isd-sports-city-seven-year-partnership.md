## **Infrastructure Expansion: RGS Guildford Dubai Inks Monumental Seven-Year Extension with ISD Dubai Sports City**

The architectural and high-performance framework of UAE school sports has locked in a defining long-term foundational pillar. **Royal Grammar School Guildford Dubai (RGS Dubai)** has announced a massive seven-year partnership renewal with **ISD Dubai Sports City**, securing elite, multi-venue facility access that will continue to shape the school’s athletic development programs until well into the next decade.

Building on an initial collaboration that began when RGS Dubai first opened its gates in 2021, the new agreement ensures that the school's burgeoning sporting pipeline retains continuous, dedicated access to one of the most technologically advanced sports campuses in the Middle East.

### **An Elite Midweek Training Base**

  
While competitive matches routinely take place directly on the primary RGS Dubai campus, the massive scale of the school's sports academy requires an elite external training footprint to sustain its momentum. Under this renewed seven-year deal, ISD Dubai Sports City serves as the ultimate high-performance midweek training base, allowing RGS to run simultaneous, high-volume squad training sessions that cannot be accommodated on a single campus.

The partnership also cements the iconic **“RGS Rugby Park”** venue at ISD as a premier, high-traffic hub for specialized skill-development blocks. The elite footprint has also paved the way for the school to anchor tier-one regional showcase events when needed, including senior sports days and full-scale **BSME (British Schools in the Middle East) Games** championships.

### **The Blueprint: High-Volume Student Impact**

  
The operational scale of the agreement is immense. Each week, approximately **700 RGS Dubai pupils** utilize the ISD campus, transitioning seamlessly between indoor training domes, outdoor pitches, professional athletics tracks, and top-tier tennis courts.

The infrastructure accommodates up to 180 pupils simultaneously, allowing the school to deliver up to three-and-a-half hours of highly optimized, timetabled physical education and performance sport training per week for every child.

"A truly well-rounded education extends far beyond the classroom, and this collaboration has created exceptional opportunities for our pupils to develop confidence, resilience, teamwork, and character through sport," said **Matthew Pearce**, Principal of Royal Grammar School Guildford Dubai. "This is an inspiring environment that reflects the ambition and values of RGS Dubai."

### **Shaping the Next Generation at Scale**

  
For ISD Dubai Sports City, which currently serves as a vital community sporting hub for over 8,000 students across the region, partnering with a forward-thinking school like RGS allows them to fulfill their high-performance developmental mission at absolute scale.

"Sport shapes young people in ways that go far beyond performance, and the schools we partner with are how we fulfill that belief," stated **Daniels Petrovs**, CEO of ISD Dubai Sports City. "RGS Dubai has been part of this community since 2021, and this renewal is a commitment to deepening that relationship and to what we are collectively building here for the long term."

By blending 500 years of British heritage from its parent school in the UK with the world-class training facilities of ISD—alongside their own premium on-campus assets like a FIFA-approved pitch and a 25-meter swimming pool—RGS Dubai has locked in its status as a premier destination for youth sporting excellence in the UAE.

## **The RGS & ISD Partnership: Fast Facts**

  

  
-   🤝 **The Deal:** 7-Year Strategic Extension (Extending a partnership launched in 2021).
  
-   📈 **Weekly Student Volume:** ~700 pupils utilizing the ISD campus every week.
  
-   🏟️ **Core Venues Secured:** Indoor Training Domes, Outdoor Pitches, Athletics Tracks, Tennis Courts, and the famous **RGS Rugby Park**.
  
-   🚀 **Primary Function:** High-performance weekday squad training, PE lessons, and major regional events like the **BSME Games**.
