## **Ultimate All-Rounders: Royal Grammar School Guildford Dubai Clinches BSME U13 Green Title**

The **BSME U13 Games Green 2026** delivered a spectacular weekend of elite junior sport as ten premier academies from across the Gulf clashed in Dubai. When the final points were tallied, it was the exceptional all-round depth of **Royal Grammar School (RGS) Guildford Dubai** that stood tallest, securing the coveted **Overall Championship** title.

RGS Guildford Dubai built their championship campaign on unmatched consistency. They claimed **1st place in Girls' Netball**, took runner-up spots in both **Girls' Football and Boys' Football**, and secured a top-four finish in **Boys' Athletics**. This relentless podium hunt across multiple sports gave them the decisive edge in a fiercely contested leaderboard.

### **Muscat and South View Ignite the Podiums**

  
The international challenge was led by the **British School Muscat (BSM)**, who claimed an impressive **2nd place overall**. The Oman-based powerhouse completely owned the track and field, executing a flawless double by winning **1st place in both Girls' and Boys' Athletics**, with BSM’s **Easa** capturing the Boys' Athletics MVP award.

Completing the overall podium in **3rd place** was **South View School**. The Dubai squad saved their finest form for the grass, turning on a masterclass of clinical counter-attacking play to win **1st place in Boys' Football**.

### **Court and Pitch Dramatics**

  
The tournament brackets produced spectacular individual sport champions. **Jebel Ali School (JAS)** lit up the pitch to win **1st place in Girls' Football** after a flawless display of high-pressing tactical play. On the volleyball courts, **British School Dhahran** showed incredible composure under pressure to claim **1st place in Boys' Volleyball**.
