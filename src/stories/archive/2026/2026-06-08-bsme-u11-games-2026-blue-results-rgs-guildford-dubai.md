## **Blueprint for Success: Royal Grammar School Guildford Dubai Claims Coveted BSME U11 Blue Championship**

The **BSME U11 Games 2026** Blue Division provided a platform for some of the most exciting young prospects in the region to emerge. When the final whistle blew, it was the clinical execution of **Royal Grammar School (RGS) Guildford Dubai** that claimed the ultimate prize, securing **1st place overall** to write their name on the prestigious trophy.

RGS Guildford Dubai’s campaign was marked by tactical elite-level execution on the grass and the track. They marched to a dominant **1st place finish in Boys' Football** and backed it up immediately by securing **1st place in Girls' Athletics**, heavily driven by tournament MVP **Riley**.

### **South View and Horizon Push the Pace**

  
The race for the podium was incredibly tight. **South View School** claimed an exceptional **2nd place overall** following highly consistent finishes across the board. South View showcased their prowess in the water, locking down **2nd place in Swimming**, anchored by an outstanding individual performance from Boys' Swim MVP **Adam**.

Clinching **3rd place overall** was **Horizon English School**. Horizon proved to be the absolute speed kings of the weekend, flying down the track to capture **1st place in Boys' Athletics**, powered by individual standout **Leo**. They also picked up the Boys' Football MVP crown via **Charles** and Netball MVP via **Hannah**.

### **Standout Specialisms**

The Blue Division showcased incredible diversity in winners. **Pearl British Academy** proved to be an absolute powerhouse in specific codes, stunning the field to claim **1st place in both Girls' Football and Boys' Basketball**, with **Loudi and Awni** claiming respective tournament MVP awards. Meanwhile, **Victory Heights Primary School** dominated the pool to take **1st place in Swimming**, led by the dynamic duo of **Margaux and Adhya**.

## **BSME U11 Blue Division Tier List**

-   **Overall Champions:** Royal Grammar School Guildford Dubai, UAE 🏆
-   **Girls’ Football Winners:** Pearl British Academy, UAE _(MVP: Loudi - Pearl Academy)_
-   **Boys’ Basketball Winners:** Pearl British Academy, UAE _(MVP: Awni - Pearl Academy)_
-   **Girls’ Athletics Winners:** RGS Guildford Dubai, UAE _(MVP: Riley - RGS Dubai)_
-   **Boys’ Athletics Winners:** Horizon English School, UAE _(MVP: Leo - Horizon)_
-   **Girls’ Netball Winners:** DESS Primary Academic City, UAE _(MVP: Hannah - Horizon)_
-   **Boys’ Football Winners:** RGS Guildford Dubai, UAE _(MVP: Charles - Horizon)_
-   **Swimming Winners:** Victory Heights Primary School, UAE _(MVPs: Margaux & Adhya - VHPS / Adam - South View)_
-   **Fairplay Award:** Deira International School, UAE 🤝
