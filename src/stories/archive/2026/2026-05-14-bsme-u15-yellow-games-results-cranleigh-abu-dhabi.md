## **The Capital’s Finest: Cranleigh Abu Dhabi Clinches Overall Victory at the BSME U15 Yellow Games**

The **BSME U15 Yellow Games 2025** delivered one of the most competitive weekends in recent memory. Managed by **TWEDEX**, the event saw ten elite schools battle across multiple disciplines. In a finish that came down to the wire, **Cranleigh Abu Dhabi** emerged as the Overall Champions, showcasing the depth of talent within their athletic programme.

Cranleigh’s victory was built on clinical performances in the core team sports. Their **1st place finish in the Boys’ Football** provided a massive points boost, while their podium finishes in Girls' Football and Boys' Athletics ensured they stayed ahead of a surging Arcadia British School.

### **The Battle for the Podium**

  
The race for the top spot was a two-horse race until the final events. **Arcadia British School (UAE)** secured the **Silver Medal** after a dominant showing in the individual disciplines. Arcadia proved to be the team to beat on the track and the court, taking 1st place in both **Girls’ Athletics** and **Girls’ Netball**.

Rounding out the top three was **St. Christopher’s School, Bahrain**. Known for their sporting pedigree, the Bahrain-based school took the **Bronze Medal** overall. Their success was anchored by a double victory in the specialist categories, taking 1st place in both **Girls’ Football** and **Boys’ Volleyball**.

### **Professional Standards by TWEDEX**

As with all major BSME events, the logistics were handled by **TWEDEX**. This partnership ensures that student-athletes can perform in an environment that mirrors professional sport. From the "Player of the Tournament" recognition to the seamless transition between venues, the professional touch was evident throughout the weekend.

### **Sport-Specific Winners:**

-   **Overall Champions:** Cranleigh Abu Dhabi, UAE
-   **Boys’ Football:** Cranleigh Abu Dhabi, UAE
-   **Girls’ Football:** St. Christopher's School, Bahrain
-   **Boys’ Volleyball:** St. Christopher's School, Bahrain
-   **Girls’ Athletics:** Arcadia British School, UAE
-   **Boys’ Athletics:** Deira International School, UAE
-   **Girls’ Netball:** Arcadia British School, UAE
