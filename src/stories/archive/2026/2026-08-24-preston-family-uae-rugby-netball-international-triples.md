## **Dynasty in the Making: Elle Preston Completes Unprecedented Family Treble for UAE International Sport**

In the history of Gulf sports, few family legacies can match what the Preston family has just achieved.

With the official announcement of the UAE Women’s Rugby squad set to compete in China, rising star **Elle Preston** has officially earned her call-up to the senior ladies' team. Not only is she the youngest player named to the senior squad, but she will also double up on national duties in China as part of the **UAE U18 Rugby squad**.

Her dual selection marks a historic milestone: **all three Preston siblings have now represented the United Arab Emirates at both age-category and senior open levels over the past five years.**

### **A Two-Decade Legacy in UAE Community Sport**

  
Having lived in the UAE for 20 years, the Preston family’s roots in local grassroots sport run deep. Years of early morning pitch sessions, court sessions, and unwavering commitment to regional athletics have culminated in an extraordinary hat-trick of international representation:  

  
-   🏉 **Max Preston:** UAE Rugby U20 Captain & Men’s Senior National Team
  
-   🏐 **Charlie Preston:** UAE Netball U17 Captain & Women’s Senior National Team
  
-   🏉 **Elle Preston:** UAE Rugby U18 & Women’s Senior National Team
  

  
From captaining national youth sides to wearing the UAE crest on the senior international stage across two different major sports, the trio represents the absolute pinnacle of the region’s youth sports pathway.  

### **China Bound: Elle Preston Steps onto the Big Stage**

  
At just 17, Elle’s inclusion in both the U18 and Senior Women's rugby squads traveling to China highlights her remarkable tactical maturity and physical dynamism on the pitch.

Her selection follows in the exact footsteps of her older brother Max—a formidable force and former U20 captain in UAE rugby—and sister Charlie, who established herself as one of the region's finest netball talents after captaining the U17 national team into senior international honors.

_"To have one child represent their country is a proud moment for any family, but to have all three siblings captain youth sides and earn senior international caps across rugby and netball is unprecedented in UAE youth sports history."_

As Elle prepares to take on Asia's finest in China, the Preston family legacy stands as a powerful inspiration for every young athlete growing up in the Middle Eastern grassroots system.
