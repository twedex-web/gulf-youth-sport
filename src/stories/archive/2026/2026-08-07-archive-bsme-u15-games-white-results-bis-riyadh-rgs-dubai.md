## **GYS Flashback: BIS Riyadh AW Crowned Overall Champions in Thrilling BSME U15 Games White**

In this edition of the _GYS Archive Vault_, we look back at a multi-sport continental showcase that tested the depth, versatility, and athletic endurance of the region's top international schools. The **BSME U15 Games White (2025)** brought together 11 leading academies spanning Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Qatar, Egypt, Kuwait, and Bahrain for a high-stakes, multi-discipline tournament.

When the final tally across football, netball, volleyball, and athletics was calculated, it was **British International School Riyadh – Al Waha (AW), KSA**, that stood tall at the peak of the podium to claim the title of **Overall BSME U15 Games White Champions**.

### **The Overall Podium: Saudi Arabia and UAE Lead the Way**

  
BIS Riyadh (AW) constructed their championship victory on incredible multi-sport consistency, picking up gold medal finishes in Boys' Volleyball, Boys' Athletics, and Boys' Football.

Pushing them all the way to the wire was the UAE’s **Royal Grammar School (RGS) Guildford Dubai**, who produced an remarkable overall performance to claim **2nd Place Overall (Silver)**—highlighted by a dominant Gold-medal run in Girls' Netball and Silver in Girls' Athletics. Completing a massive podium double for the Saudi capital, **British International School Riyadh – Diplomatic Quarter (DQ)** secured **3rd Place Overall (Bronze)** after capturing the Girls' Football title.
