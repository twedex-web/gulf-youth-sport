## **Splash of Class: All Six Divisional Champions Crowned in Epic DASSA Primary Swimming Finals at Hamdan**

The prestigious **Hamdan Sports Complex** became the epicenter of high-performance youth aquatics this month. The pool decks bore witness to an absolute masterclass of speed, depth, and precision during the **DASSA Primary Swimming Championships**.

Staged across two blockbuster dates—with Divisions 1-3 locking horns on **June 8th** and Divisions 4-6 taking the plunge on **June 18th**—the showcase events brought together thousands of Dubai's elite junior swimmers. Following recent calendar disruptions, the one-off championship format delivered pure tactical drama, fast pacing, and an unbelievably world-class atmosphere.

## **🌊 DIVISIONS 1-3: TIERS OF PURE DOMINANCE**

  
The opening block of gala finals established an exceptionally high technical benchmark, with overall team depth proving to be the ultimate path to silverware.  

### **Division 1: Kings' School Dubai Conquer the Field**

  
The absolute heavyweights of the top tier, **Kings' School Dubai**, put on a clinical display to walk away as the undisputed **Division 1 Overall Champions**. Their performance set the tone for the entire championship series.  

### **Division 2: Arcadia British School Asserts Authority**

  
Mirroring their spectacular multi-sport success this season, **Arcadia British School** showcased their incredible aquatics pathway, comfortably hoisting the banner as the **Division 2 Overall Champions**.  

### **Division 3: South View School Rules the Lanes**

  
In a brilliant testament to their growing swim academy, **South View School** dominated their competitive tier to claim the title of **Division 3 Overall Champions**.  

## **🏊‍♂️ DIVISIONS 4-6: THE SECOND WAVE DELIVERS SMASH HITS**

  
The momentum shifted to the second block of competition on June 18th, where another trio of school programs stamped their complete authority on the Hamdan waters.  

### **Division 4: DESS Academic City Seizes Glory**

  
Showcasing phenomenal technical execution across both individual sprints and relay transitions, **DESS Academic City** swept ahead of the chasing pack to become **Division 4 Overall Champions**.  

### **Division 5: JESS Jumeirah Wears the Crown**

  
The elite pipeline of **JESS Jumeirah** rose to the high-pressure occasion, combining tactical consistency and individual power to lift the coveted **Division 5 Overall Champions** trophy.  

### **Division 6: Deira International School Steals the Spotlight**

  
Rounding out the historic swimming campaign, **Deira International School** put together a spectacular, balanced team performance to rightfully secure the title of **Division 6 Overall Champions**.  

## **🤝 BACKSTAGE MASTERS & COMMUNITY HEROES**

  
Special individual recognition was directed to **Chris Dove** for his relentless, tireless work behind the scenes managing the complex timing arrays, entries, and HYTEK systems. Alongside Chris, a massive thank you goes out to the dedicated **DASSA Committee Members** for their exceptional coordination, strategic planning, and leadership in bringing these championships to life. Furthermore, vital volunteer and corporate support from **Hamilton Aquatics**, **Speedo**, and the **Cognita** network ensured both event blocks ran with absolute professional precision.
