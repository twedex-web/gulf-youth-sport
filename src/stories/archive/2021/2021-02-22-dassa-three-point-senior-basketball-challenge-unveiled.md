**Following on from the immense success of the DASSA 1km Virtual Running Challenge, the Senior Basketball Three-Point Challenge has arrived!**

For this challenge students will work in groups of three to score as many three-pointers as possible in a one-minute period.

Excitingly, the virtual challenge is open to all DASSA schools and also encourages other schools in the UAE and around the world to send in their entries.

Entries must be submitted and shared by schools rather than individual students, but don't forget to use the hashtag #DASSA3point to show DASSA and us how you're preparing.
