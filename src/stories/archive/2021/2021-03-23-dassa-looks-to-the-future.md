**At DASSA's Town Hall meeting on Sunday the committee shared its thoughts on a host of important issues regarding PE and sport in Dubai and the UAE.**

**The return of competitive sport was at the forefront with September and the beginning of the new school year touted as a potential return date to competition by the DASSA PE experts. However everything of course remains subject to KHDA guidelines and approval.**

**You can rewatch all the key points below and discover more about the expansion of the DASSA Select program, online challenges and how teachers can become more involved with the not-for-profit DASSA organisation.**
