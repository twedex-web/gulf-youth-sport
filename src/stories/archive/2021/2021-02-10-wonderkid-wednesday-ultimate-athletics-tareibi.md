Here's a go-getter we'll be seeing plenty of in the future.

Tareibi won three medals at Ultimate Athletics' Racenight and the 14 year-old GEMS International School student is impressive in both track and field events.

He won gold in the 100m with the swift time of 11.76 and also finished first in the high jump clearing a height of 1.6m. In addition a silver medal in the 200m capped off a fantastic night.

Tareibi earned selection to the Gothenburg Youth Games which were unfortunately cancelled this year but we're sure he'll be at the next event!

Ultimate Athletics director Lisa Campbell described him as a 'very talented sprinter with great vertical power for jumping.' The complete package!
