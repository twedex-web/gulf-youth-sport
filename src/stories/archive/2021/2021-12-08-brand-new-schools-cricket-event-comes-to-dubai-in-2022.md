**The Dubai Schools Games Cricket Championships are set to arrive next term with U15 teams battling it out to become the city's best side.**

U15 boys school teams can be entered now to secure your place and players can be added over the coming weeks prior to the start date.

  

Group stage matches will take place at GEMS Modern Academy, starting on January 9th through to February 25th and will take place between 4.30pm – 6.30pm. Each team will play a minimum of three matches (approximately one game every two weeks) and the top teams will qualify for the knockout stages.

  
Entries will be accepted online via the DSG school portal [here](https://dev.dubaischoolsgames.ae/school) and more information is available on the website [www.dubaischoolsgames.ae](http://www.dubaischoolsgames.ae/).  

This event is endorsed by the ECB (Emirates Cricket Board) and is free of charge to enter so don’t miss out for your school to have the chance to become the ‘Dubai Schools Games Cricket Champions 2022’

  

The Dubai Schools Games is an Official Initiative of the Dubai Sports Council and all data is protected and owned by the Dubai Sports Council.
