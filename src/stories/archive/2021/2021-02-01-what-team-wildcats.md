**GEMS Wellington International School is taking a walk on the wild side.**

Since the start of the academic year the students at GEMS WIS were tasked with developing a brand for PE and school sport, with the main aim of developing an environment that all members of the community could aspire to be a part of. The brand has been designed by the students for the students and we can't wait to see what's next for the newly named Wildcats.

**Director of Sport Hannah Underhill** said it was a positive way to bring the GEMS community together.

"Competitive school sport has been halted for almost a year now, but that doesn’t mean that the fire burns any less bright! Champions are made in the off season, and right now our focus is on building a sporting culture that all members of our school community are inspired to be a part of!" she said.

Excitingly [GYS Sports Media Course](/2020/09/21/gysmediacourse/) graduate and GEMS WIS student Kaira Pal used her skills to write fantastic article for this week's offical launch of the new logo and name. Read it below:

_We, GEMS Wellington International School, are proud to announce the launch of our new PE and Sport_ _logo and brand. Over the past 15 years, WIS has been constantly aiming for sporting excellence, and we_ _now want to take this to the next level by acknowledging everyone and ensuring every individual of our_ _community feels like a part of the team. We aim to reflect our morals and core values through this new_  
_logo._

_Over the course of the COVID-19 pandemic, the students at WIS were tasked with putting forward their_ _ideas on what they believed best represented the values and ethos of the school’s sporting culture, as_ _part of the PE and Sport Branding Project. Every student from year two to year 13 spent time researching_ _the value and importance of their PE lessons, selecting the core values, and using their creative skills to_  
_create this embodiment of the spirit and camaraderie that we strive towards._

_Director of Sport, Mrs_ _Hannah Underhill, believes that this project will reshape the future of sports and will create a legacy for_  
_the future students.__We envision that this new logo, inspired by the spirit of the school’s sporting culture, will enhance the_ _motivation, energy and spirit of the individuals within the school. We want this logo to express the_ _success of any sporting endeavor, and we are so excited to unveil it! We eagerly hope that it’s new look_ _and feel will enhance physical education at WIS, inspiring our students to push themselves and join in!_

_This synergy in creating a representation of what we desire our future to look like, draws from the core_ _values of sports: teamwork, dedication and persistence. We have altered our logo to reflect who we are_ _today and to symbolize our dynamic future. However we still retain the logo’s core elements - the_ _shield: a representation of strength and resilience- we are able to defend throughout our trials and_ _triumphs._

_We have also kept the crown: gleaming in a shade of gold - to symbolise our nobility and_ _confidence. Our heads are held high with our pride of what we have achieved and what is to come._

_After a careful consideration, we selected a new logo that reflects a more__modern look and captures everyone's vision. We are pleased to announce_ _that the winner of our new logo is Lara Moutran from Year 9._ _Congratulations, Lara! We are now going to be known as the WIS Wildcats!_

_The name itself describes the individuals at WIS - confident, powerful and_ _courageous. Lara said: ‘it's an honour to help create and design the new sports_ _logo.’ She thought it was an amazing opportunity to use the students' creative_ _brains and to see how the design has evolved from the initial draft to the_ _fantastic design it is now._

_Today marks the beginning of a new exciting chapter for all the sports enthusiasts. You don’t have to be_ _a part of a sports team in order to be a WIS Wildcat, it’s all about the passion and dedication you put_ _forward, whether you are a player or spectator._

_Are you ready for a new beginning, WIS Wildcats?_
