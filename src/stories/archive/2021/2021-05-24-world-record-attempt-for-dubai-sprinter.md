**17-year old Giacamo De Lorenzi is a young man on a mission. We caught up with the Athletes in Schools sprinter ahead of his Guinness World Record attempt at GEMS World Academy this weekend.**

Giacomo has been training to attempt to break the world record for the most mountain climbers in one minute for the last six months as part of his training programme with AIS Athletics.

The current record is 80 reps and the young athlete will be looking to write his name in the history books by beating that this week.

Everyone at Gulf Youth Sport wishes Giacomo the very best of luck is his attempt!
