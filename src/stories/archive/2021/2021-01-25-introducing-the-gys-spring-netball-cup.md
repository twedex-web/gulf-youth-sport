**GYS SPRING NETBALL CUP**

**February 26-27th 2021, The Sevens Stadium**

**🏐 Squads of up to 12 players**

**🏐 U8, U10, U12, U14 and U16 age groups**

**🏐 2 divisions for the U12,U14 and U16 age groups–Performance and Development**

**🏐 Minimum of 4 matches for every team**

**🏐 Team Fee: One team = AED 925 per team or** **Multiple teams = AED 795 per team**

**🏐 To register your interest: [https://gulfyouthsport.com/springnetballcup/](/springnetballcup/)**

**The GYS Spring Netball Cup is arriving in Dubai on February 26 and 27 and you're invited to bring your team together and join in.**

We're ecstatic to partner with Active Netball to give young netballers a chance to compete on some of the country's best courts at The Sevens Stadium.

Squads of up to 12 players can be entered and teams will only be required on one day of the tournament. Friday will see the U14 and U16 age groups hold centre stage, with U8, U10 and U12 divisions competition the following day.

To ensure an even competition the U12, U14 and U16 will have two tiers and coaches can put in a preference for whether they would like to be entered into the performance or development division.

All Dubai Sports Council protocols will be strictly adhered to, ensuring young netballers can simply get on with doing what they do best – performing on the netball court.
