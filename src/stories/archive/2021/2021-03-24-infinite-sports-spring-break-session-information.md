Whether football, cricket, gymnastics, swimming or multi-sports is your thing, Infinite Sports has something to suit for your spring break.

Take a look at the flyers below for further details. Infinite Sports is also offering a discount on its multi-sport camp, with instructions on how to redeem below.

**Details for the Infinite Multi-Activity Sessions:**

  
Dates: 28th March -1st April and 4th April - 8th April

Locations: Jumeirah Baccalaureate School; Horizon International School; Universal American School;  Dwight School Dubai; Uptown International School.

Activities include:Basketball, dodgeball, team building, art&crafts, gymnastics and more!

Price: GYS 25% Discount Price: AED 669 per week (excl. VAT)

Discount Code : GYS25

To book now:
