ISD Athletics, the UAE’s newest Athletics Academy for youth from the ages of four and above will open its doors this week at Dubai Sports City.

The academy is led by Andrew Turner, three-time GB Olympian, European and Commonwealth gold medalist and world record holder.

ISD Athletics Academy will offer Dubai athletes a new, world-class experience in athletics. As part of its launch celebrations, free lessons will be offered throughout the opening week led by Turner, former Team GB sprinter and current Nigerian national team athlete Alex Al-Ameen and Italian national team sprinter Antonio Infantino.

"Dubai is one of the world’s most driven in the world of sport, with sports development at the core of its ambitions. ISD Athletics  
Academy will bring an unparalleled experience to the youth of the UAE, providing them with first-rate programs in all the disciplines of track and field, as well as providing top athletes with a pathway to pursue professional careers in athletics through college scholarships in the US. We look forward to welcoming Dubai’s youth to ISD Athletics at Dubai Sports City starting this week, "Turner said.

Athletes of all levels are encourage to come out this week to meet Turner and his team on the track at Dubai Sports City and to experience the sport of athletics, learn new skills and compete with yourself.

For more information, please contact Inspiratus Sports District on 058 285 8239.
