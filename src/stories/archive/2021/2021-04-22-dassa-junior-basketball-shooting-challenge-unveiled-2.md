**Following on from the immense success of [DASSA's Senior Basketball Challenge](https://www.instagram.com/p/CNCdmTaIgGU/), DASSA's Junior Basketball Shooting Challenge has arrived!**

For this challenge students will work in groups of three to score as many baskets as possible in a one-minute period.

This challenge is open to primary students, and all entries are due in by Thursday **May 13, 2021.**

Excitingly, the virtual challenge is open to all DASSA schools and also encourages other schools in the UAE and around the world to send in their entries.

To enter, tag DASSA on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/dassasports/?hl=en) or [Twitter](https://twitter.com/dassa_sports) and use the hashtag #DASSAbasketball, stating the school or academy involved and year level. Alternatively, email the videos to [admin@dassasport.org](/admin@dassasport.org). Good luck!
