**In 2021 we look forward to hosting the GYS Spring Football Cup, GYS Spring Netball Cup and Dubai Youth Basketball League. At GYS giving young and emerging talents the chance to shine is important and these three competitions allow a variety of age groups and skill levels to have their chance to compete.**
