**Dedicated is the word to describe AIS Athletics young gun Lucian.**

The nine year-old and he is a very keen footballer, athlete and swimmer.

He has achieved many awards at King's School Dubai including 2019 and 2020 athlete of the year and winning the 400m relay at DAPSA competition while representing his school.

At school he’s recognized for his sports and is always in the squads to compete against other schools.

He hold a record for sprinting at sports day which helps him on the football field, saluting when his side won the league in 2019.

Swimming three times a week, he is constantly achieving a new personal best and there is plenty of potential especially when his growth spurt kicks in.

Lucian's passion is with athletics and he wants to aim higher and be challenged by older kids.

Lucian is very committed and dedicated, not missing a session unless he’s sick or abroad. Even when invited to a birthday party, he tries to find a way to be part of both!

Coaches and staff at AIS Athletics describe him as "really mature when it comes to his sports, very active and with lots of energy."
