Students' safety is the top priority at MPAC with coaches in face shields and students wearing face masks.

The academy's summer skills camp is a three hour camp designed to return students to tip-top shape so they can again be basketball ready.

The camp includes fitness training for improved health and cardio, professional basketball training for an improved skill set and leadership training to increase the confidence and motivation of students.

All students will maintain two meter social distancing, equipment will be thoroughly cleaned between programs and the facility maintenance team will be available to ensure the safety and health of all staff and students.

Students aged 5-18 can participate. See the below image for full information.
