Netball trials for the DAPSA and DASSA Select Squads are underway with supreme skills on show.

Kings' School Al Barsha's Carly Lewis is coach of the squad and said it is a fantastic program.

"This is a great opportunity for the girls to really showcase their skills," she said.

"The process and the aim of the DAPSA Select teams is to bring all the best girls in year six together and make two squads."
