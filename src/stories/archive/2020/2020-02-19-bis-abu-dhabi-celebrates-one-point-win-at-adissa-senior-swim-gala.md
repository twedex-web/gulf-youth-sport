It all came down to the final count.

The British International School Abu Dhabi and The British School Al Khubairat led the race to take top honours at the ADISSA Swim Gala.

After magnificent efforts in the pool BIS just pipped their counterparts, celebrating an epic 256-255 victory in the overall tally

Winning swim coach Stephen Monaghan was ecstatic to win the competition at BIS' Sports Academy Pool.

"I am super proud of the students who have achieved a very big honour for the school and themselves," he said.

Raha International College picked up third position with Brighton College and Al Yasmina Academy following in the rankings.
