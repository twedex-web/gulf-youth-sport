A four week league for six-aside teams is coming to Dubai from the organisers of the popular Girls Community Football tournaments.

What started as a day tournament and had hundreds of girls take part has grown to a competition beginning on October 30th at GEMS World Academy. Matches will take place from 8am-12 noon, with games following on November 6th, 13th and 20th.

U11s and U13s are invited to take part and all are welcome–whether you're a team from a club, school or purely led by a football loving parent!

Still utilising a round-robin format, teams are guaranteed to compete in three 20 minute games giving athletes plenty of match play to hone their skills.

U9s haven't been left out with a fun tournament expected before the end of the year.
