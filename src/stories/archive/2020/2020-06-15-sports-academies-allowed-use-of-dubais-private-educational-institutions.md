Another major step has been taken in allowing sports academies to gradually move towards normal functioning in Dubai.

Dubai Sports Council announced sports academies can now use Dubai's private educational institutions to resume their coaching and training activities in accordance with safety protocols issued by Dubai's Supreme Committee of Crisis and Disaster Management

With so many academies relying on the pristine facilities at schools, their continued closure hampered a return. But these courts and fields will slowly have participants again, all following social distancing and hygiene regulations.
