In welcome news to every under-12 athlete, Dubai Sports Council has lifted all age restrictions allowing every age group to resume sports activity.   Now those aged under 12 and over 60 can again take part in sport and join the many athletes already training under strict hygiene arrangements.   As facilities at schools can now also be used, we look forward to seeing siblings join each other at academy trainings!
