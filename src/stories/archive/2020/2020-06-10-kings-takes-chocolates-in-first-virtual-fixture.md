Congratulations to Kings' School Al Barsha for taking the chocolates in the first virtual fixture. Every individual effort from the students was compiled and resulted in a 6-3 win against Spain's Laude Newton College.   Jasper Girven gave Kings' a huge boost and was named his school's MVP while Marcos Lopez was sensational for Laude Newton.   The winner was decided from the best of nine rounds and the full results are below.
