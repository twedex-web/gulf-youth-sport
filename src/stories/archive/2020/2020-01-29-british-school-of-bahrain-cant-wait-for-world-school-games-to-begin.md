The athletes at the British School of Bahrain are struggling to contain their excitement in anticipation of the under-11 World School Games beginning tomorrow.

Having just jumped off a short flight to Dubai where the Games are taking place, enthusiastic student Cameron explains why tomorrow's athletics competition at Dubai Sports City can not come soon enough.
