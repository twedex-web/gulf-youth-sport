The British International School, Abu Dhabi has a documentary maker in their ranks. Jasmine Pitman is just 14 but has produced an inspiring short documentary on how her father developed a love of running and improved his health.   All the interviewing, filming and editing was done by Jasmine as part of the [GYS free sports journalism course.](/2020/05/19/gys-free-sports-journalism-course-for-12-18-year-olds/) She combined all the skills learnt across the eight modules in a piece that is so impressive to watch. Well done Jasmine!
