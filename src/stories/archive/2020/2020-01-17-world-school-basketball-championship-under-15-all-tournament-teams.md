_Hoop Mountain coaches and World School Basketball Championship technical directors Julian King and Ryan Yates pinpointed some of the most influential performers at the December tournament. See what made the players stand out below:_ 

**  
#1 Filip Popivoda (Most Valuable Player)**  
**School:** Dubai College  
**Grade:** 9  
**Jersey:** #11  
**Position:** SG/SF  
**Height:** 186cm  
**Strengths:** -Long/athletic wing  
\-Strong ball handler and passer who makes good decisions  
\-Good outside shooter  
\-Relentless attacking the basket  
\-Solid perimeter defender and plays passing lanes well  
\-Good rebounder  
\-Tough/gritty/plays extremely hard on both ends  
**Favourite basketball player:** Nikola Jokic

  

**#2 Joshua Aguinaldo**  
**School:** GEMS Winchester  
**Grade:** N/A  
**Jersey:** #11  
**Position:** PG  
**Strengths:** -Strong, athletic guard  
\-Good shooting off the dribble and pick and roll  
\-Gets to the basket at will  
\-Great playmaking ability  
\-Great scorer in transition

  

**#3 Nathaniel Williams**  
**School:** Dwight school  
**Jersey:** #13  
**Position:** PF/C  
**Height:** 187cm  
**Strengths:** -Long/Athletic Big/High motor  
\-Excellent rebounder  
\-Sprints the floor well  
\-Strong defensive presence and shot blocker  
\-Finishes strong around the rim  
**Favourite basketball player:** Lebron James  
**Hobbies:** Basketball and playing video games

  

**#4 Amal Dhanesh**  
**School:** Dubai College  
**Grade:** 9  
**Jersey:** #24  
**Position:** SG/PG  
**Height:** 178CM  
**Strengths:** -Big guard  
\-Always plays under control  
\-Good shooter  
\-Rebounds well from guard position  
\-Very good on ball defender  
**Favourite basketball player:** Kyrie Irving  
**Hobbies:** Football and playing the saxophone

  

#5 Amin Mansour  
**School:** Dwight School  
**Grade:** 9  
**Jersey:** #10  
**Position:** SF  
**Height:** 180cm  
**Strengths:** -Very athletic  
\-Finishes well around the basket  
\-Decent midrange shooter  
\-Runs the floor well  
\-Good rebounder  
**Favourite basketball player:** Michael Jordan  
**Hobbies:** Basketball

  

#6 Oskar Granberg (RAHA International School)   
**School:** RAHA International School  
**Jersey:** #20  
**Position:** PF/C  
**Strengths:** -Excellent offensive rebounder
