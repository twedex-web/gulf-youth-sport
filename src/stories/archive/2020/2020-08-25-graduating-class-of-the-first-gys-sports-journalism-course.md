A big congratulations to nine sterling students who completed GYS' media and sports journalism course with flying colours. The nine budding sports journalists covered the sports they love as rugby, football, netball, diving and athletics all took centre stage.   So much effort went into their work from match reports to news bulletins, commentary and short videos, all while learning about a career in the media industry.    They deserve a huge round of applause for what they produced! Don't be surprised if these faces pop up when you're watching sport in the future.   Graduating class (clockwise from top left): Lauren Hibbert, Felipe Wyss, Danielle Guerin, Erin D'Oliveira, Jasmine Pitman, David Lozano, Daniel D'Oliveira and Lily Hatton
