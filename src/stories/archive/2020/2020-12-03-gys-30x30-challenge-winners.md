**A bit of friendly competition has spurred on our inaugural winners in the GYS 30X30 Challenge.**

**Using the [Movement Wins](https://www.movement-wins.com/) activity tracker, Jebel Ali was seen to top the leaderboards each week which led to their overall victory. Resultantly, the school will have its basketball team kitted by [ANTA Sportswear](https://www.facebook.com/ANTAsportswear/).**

**Students, teachers and parents all made up the supportive community that burned thousands of calories through purposeful activity across the month. We hope this spurs everyone on to keep up the great work across winter.**
