There were some huge numbers shown on the first GYS 30X30 Challenge leaderboards!

Jebel Ali School is enjoying a healthy lead atop the schools table, with a combined calorie total of 28 885, but [Movement Wins](https://www.movement-wins.com/) are crunching the numbers and a team effort could still see Jebel Ali overtaken.

Female and male leaderboards include teachers, students AND parents so you'll have to wait to see who's topping each category and in line for some [ANTA Sportswear](https://www.facebook.com/ANTAsportswear/) prizes.

Well done everyone, and keep up that purposeful exercise!
