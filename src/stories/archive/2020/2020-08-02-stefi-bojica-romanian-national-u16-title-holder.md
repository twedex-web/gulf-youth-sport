Huge congratulations must be sent towards CF Tennis Academy's wonder product Stefi Bojica. The tennis ace held aloft the trophy at the Romanian U16 National Titles, after defeating Alexia Todoni 7-5 6-3 in the final.

Bojica has been a member of CF Tennis Academy for a decade and continues to be a source of pride for the academy and the Gulf sporting region as a whole.

The road to victory forced her to produce some stellar tennis, and Bojica defeated the competition's second seed on her way towards the final at the U16 National Titles.

Bojica came out firing after the COVID-19 break and won silverware at both the Cupa Galaxy U16 Singles in Bucharest and the U16 TCB Champions Bowl in Romania. She'll next compete at the National Juniors next week. Good luck!
