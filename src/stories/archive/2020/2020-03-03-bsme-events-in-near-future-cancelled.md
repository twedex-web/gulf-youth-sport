The British Schools in the Middle East Dance Invitational scheduled to be held from April 16-18 is the latest event to be officially cancelled. Eight schools were scheduled to compete at the event at Cranleigh Abu Dhabi which will no longer go ahead.   This joins both the BSME U11 Small and Large Games which have been called off due to KHDA guidelines aimed to prevent the spread of Covid-19. Schools are being asked to cancel any large school gatherings including any international trips as well as sporting events held both within schools and externally.For more information head to the [KHDA website.](https://www.khda.gov.ae/en/studentssafety)
