The next Super 6s Touch league will start on September 8 and with a minimum age of 12, whole families can play together.

It's non-contact and with seperate leagues for men, women and mixed–as well as social and competitive– there is a level each individual should feel comfortable competing at.

School teams from JESS, DESC, Dubai College and Kings' School have previously formed teams and registrations are open for the start on Tuesday. Check out the flyer below for more information.
