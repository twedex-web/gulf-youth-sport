**After 40 days and close to 750 entries, the winners of the DASSA 1km Virtual Running Challenge have been announced.**

**DASSA vice-chairman Niall Statham was ecstatic with the huge volume of students and schools that got behind the initiative encouraging primary school students to remain active.**

**"I'd just like to thank everyone who's participated in the DASSA Virtual 1km Run. It's been a massive success," he said.**

**"It's been great to see people returning with not just one entry, but coming back time and time again and improving their times."**

**A wide spread of schools shown on the leaderboards highlighted the breadth of the competition in Dubai.**

**Here are the top 10 times in each category–browse through the names as you're sure to spot your school or a friend on there!**

**Congratulations to all those that made the final leaderboards, and to every student, parent, teacher and academy for supporting the competition.**
