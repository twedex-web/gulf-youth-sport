DASSA Select's netball squad lined up against some of the best netballers in their age group in Dubai.

Meeting DNA Netball Club in the final week of the Select program, co-coach Alana Clark said the teams relished the test.

"We've just completed our final fixture which was with the DNA club," she said.

"These girls are very elite players for their age so it was great to see them on court against the year eights."
