Great Britain sprinter Reece Prescod is gunning for a spot in TEAM GB's athletic team for the upcoming Tokyo Olympics.

While last year's preparations were interrupted due to injury, Prescod was recently in Dubai for a training camp and Gulf Youth Sport spoke to him while he was attending the [U11 World School Games](http://wsgevents.com/) at Dubai Sports City.
