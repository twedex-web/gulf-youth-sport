Dubai College could yet become an even stronger force in UAE school sport.They've invested in [Coach Logic](/2020/09/24/connecting-collaborating-and-analysing-with-coach-logic/) – software designed to improve collaboration and communication between teammates.

Dubai College Head of Sports Performance Justin Walsh explained how the school is looking to implement the technology across a range of sports.  
The program which uses video to enhance performance is hitting the Middle East and an introductory offer for schools in the region means those who sign up for five sports receive the academic package FREE.
