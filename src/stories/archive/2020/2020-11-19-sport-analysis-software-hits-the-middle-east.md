Here’s how Abingdon School in England is using Coach Logic to encourage its young athletes to develop their knowledge and present it in front of professional players, as well as their peers.

The sports technology, including an academic package, is now available in the UAE.

Using the technology of [Coach Logic](https://www.coach-logic.com/) on any device or operating system athletes can log in, review short snippets of a match and note down their thoughts for team mates, or even just their coach to see.

The innovate software, including an academic package, is now available in the region.

**As the ease of Coach Logic hits the Middle East, an introductory offer for schools in the region means those who sign up for five sports receive the academic package FREE.
