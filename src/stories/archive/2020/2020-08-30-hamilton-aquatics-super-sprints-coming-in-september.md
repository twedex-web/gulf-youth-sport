Hamilton Aquatics has been given approval from Dubai Sports Council to hold competitions again!

The academy is overjoyed to announce it will hold its Super Sprints meet at Hamdan Sports Complex on 18th September. Ages 11 and over from a variety of clubs will be taking part. There's more competition to come with a series of Race Nights to follow.

"Our events team have been working hard on putting together strict measures and procedures to ensure safe and socially distanced events can take place. ⁣This is great news for swimming and sport in Dubai. Thank you to @dubaisc (Dubai Sports Council) for your hard work and support," Hamilton Aquatics wrote on Facebook.⁣

We can't wait to see further details unveiled about the event!
