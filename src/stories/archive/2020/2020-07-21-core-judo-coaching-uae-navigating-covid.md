The social benefits of academy trainings can be as important as the physical activity itself. Core Judo Coaching UAE head coach Aaron Turner said his students are just as excited to see their friends as they are to be back at classes.

Having opened the academy in January and experienced a testing six months, Turner spoke to GYS about the constant need for initiative.
