Like every other sporting league in the world, DASSA's regular season has been thrown into turmoil. But this has not stopped the organisation creating an initiative to unite schools through sport.

In partnership with Gulf Youth Sport, both organisations want to ensure everyone has access to resources that can help them stay physically active, and therefore aid mental health in times of home schooling.

We'll be teaming up with academies across the region to bring you short exercise videos that can easily be completed at home.

All sporting, fitness and performing arts academies are encouraged to be involved by filling out their interest using [this form.](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdy4RtFKK7NeD7AbuVwVbab-W1tVKuOCttlN-PJlXDU6jYpMA/viewform)

If you complete the challenges in the videos let us know by using the hashtags **#ActiveTogetherDXB.**

Let's work together to keep kids in the region active!
