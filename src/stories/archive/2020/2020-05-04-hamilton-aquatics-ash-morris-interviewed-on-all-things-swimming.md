Hamilton Aquatics Director of Swimming Ash Morris is an expert on the UAE swim scene. He came across to Dubai more than a decade ago on a two year contract and is now in his 11th year at Hamilton Aquatics.

With so much experience behind him, listen to Morris speak about how to stay in peak condition during lockdown, why the USA are so dominant in the sport and his take on the developments of the [International Swimming League](https://isl.global/).

Locally he talked about the most talented swimmer he's coached and the hardest working swimmer he's ever seen.
