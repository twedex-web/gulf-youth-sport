School's back and so is PE!

After many directors of sport and PE teachers spent their holidays creating plans to ensure a safe return to PE, students are now benefiting with a chance to stretch their limbs in the gym. Dance and gymnastics were tipped to be popular and have already featured heavily. Check out what PE has looked like at some schools below.
