Dubai English Speaking College proved too much for Jumeirah English Speaking School in Monday night's DASSA under-19 competition.

Playing on their home ground the DESC boys opened up with the first two tries of the match with JESS never able to recover from the early deficit.

The final 47-12 scoreline leaves DESC brimming with confidence heading into its next clash against The English College Dubai while JESS looks to learn from the experience to improve for the remainder of the season.
