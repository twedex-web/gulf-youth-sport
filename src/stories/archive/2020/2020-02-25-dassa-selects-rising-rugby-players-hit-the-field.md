More than a month's preparation led to the toughest and most promising young rugby players in the region meeting each other on the field.

Competitive coaches made sure the 10-a-side clash held plenty of meaning, as the DASSA Select squads Dubai Barbarians and Dubai Lions met each other in a classy match.

Former New Zealand representative [Apollo Perelini](https://aprsa.com/) handed out the coveted guernseys to the players and the match was the culmination of the representative players' hard work cross the last six weeks.
