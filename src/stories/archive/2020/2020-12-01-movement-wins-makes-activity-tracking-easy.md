Movement Wins brought its activity tracking expertise to the GYS 30X30 Challenge. By linking up with heart rate monitors of Garmin, Fitbit and Polar watches, the company painted a picture of what the Dubai school community was doing during November.

Purposeful exercise is the cornerstone for Movement Wins, but that is whatever each individual makes it based on their own capabilities and schedules.

We chatted to Movement Wins co-founder Joff Bunt to find out more about why accountability via Movement Wins helps many stay on track.
