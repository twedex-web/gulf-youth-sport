Cameron Henry-Thorne is proving to be a very strong, athletic individual who always tries hard and wants to improve - she absolutely loves her running!

When she is not training with Athletes In Schools she is on the sports field somewhere with either Touch Rugby or in the gym training with Aspire Gymnastics (tumbling and gym as well as aerial silks).

Last year she was awarded the Athlete of the year for the year five girls at DESS and the year before that she was awarded the Touch Rugby Player of the year for her year group.

She represented her school at DASSA in year four for the relay and last year in year five for the 70m sprint and relay. She was the fastest girl in year five last year.
