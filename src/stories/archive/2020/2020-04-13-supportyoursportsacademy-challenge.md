Sports academies are struggling financially in the region and these pressures have no end in sight. With this in mind, GYS has started #SupportYourSportsAcademy to keep these organisations in the limelight and give them a platform while their physical doors must remain shut.

It is important for sport in the Gulf that these academies can open in strong shape when sport resumes. For more information on #SupportYourSportsAcademy, [head here](/2020/04/07/support-your-sports-academy/).

## The Challenge

  
This is a fun way for athletes of any sport, as well as their parents and broader community to be involved and give a shout out to their sports academy.

We’re looking for the most creative way to land a ball into a bucket. Whether it’s a football, cricket ball, hockey puck or even a tea bag into a mug – show off your skills and send your video through to our [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/gulfyouthsport2) or [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/gulfyouthsport/?hl=en) page to we can feature it.

Tag your friends and put the call out to them to take the challenge and share posts with the hashtag #SupportYourSportsAcademy to send the message far and wide.

Entries will be posted out on our Facebook and Instagram pages as well as pooled together right here, before the winner is decided by a public vote.
