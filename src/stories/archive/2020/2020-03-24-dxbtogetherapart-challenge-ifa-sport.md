_Like every other sporting league in the world, DASSA’s regular season has been thrown into turmoil. But this has not stopped the organisation creating an initiative to unite schools through sport._

_I__n partnership with Gulf Youth Sport, both organisations want to ensure everyone has access to resources that can help them stay physically active, and therefore aid mental health in times of home schooling.__We’ve teamed up with academies across the region to bring you short exercise videos that can easily be completed at home._TODAY'S challenge comes from International Football Academy, challenging you to complete as many heel taps on a football in 30 seconds.

All sporting, fitness and performing arts academies are encouraged to be involved by filling out their interest using [this form.](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdy4RtFKK7NeD7AbuVwVbab-W1tVKuOCttlN-PJlXDU6jYpMA/viewform)

If you complete the challenges in the videos let us know by using the hashtag **#ActiveTogetherDXB.**
