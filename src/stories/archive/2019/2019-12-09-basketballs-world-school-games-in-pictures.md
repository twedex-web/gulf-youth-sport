16 teams from across the world revelled in the opportunity to play against each other at the World School Basketball Championships held at the weekend.

Ultimately it was Dhahran High School (under-19s), Dubai International Academy (under-17s) and Dubai College (under-15s) who took out the title in each of the age groups.
