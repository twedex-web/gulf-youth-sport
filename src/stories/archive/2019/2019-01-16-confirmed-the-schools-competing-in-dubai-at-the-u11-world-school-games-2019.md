With the 2019 U11 World School Games getting underway in Dubai in just over two weeks, GYS can confirm all 20 schools from across the globe that will be fighting it out in three days of competition for the right to be crowned World Champions of school sport.

The competitors are:

-   ACS Cobham International School
-   Bahrain British School 
-   British School Netherlands 
-   Brookes School Moscow
-   CIS 
-   Corona School 
-   Dubai International Academy 
-   English International College Marbella 
-   Foremarke School 
-   GEMS First Point School 
-   GEMS Metropole School
-   GEMS Modern Academy
-   GEMS WSO 
-   Grange School Lagos 
-   Haileybury Almaty 
-   JESS Arabian Ranches 
-   JESS Jumeirah
-   Lagos Prep School 
-   St Catherine's British School 
-   St Saviour's School Ikoyi 
-   Temple School Lagos

Best of luck to all schools taking part – we look forward to welcoming you to Dubai at the end of the month.
