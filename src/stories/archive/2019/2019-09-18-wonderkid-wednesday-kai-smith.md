### Kai Smith of It's Just Cricket is our first Wonderkid Wednesday for the 2019/20 season.

He scored an unbeaten 50 runs vs [Darjeeling Cricket](https://www.instagram.com/DarjeelingCricket/) earlier this week. Kai will be looking to continue his good form as the first set of six ECB games take place across the UAE this weekend.

Congratulations and well done!
