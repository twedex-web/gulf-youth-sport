The finalists for the 2019 UAE Teen Sports & Fitness Awards have been announced.

372 student athletes and 53 managers are amongst the 601 nominations for categories including Best Coach and Athlete of the Year.

As well as honouring individuals, the Awards recognise the great work done for sport in the UAE by the country’s schools, academies and centres, with over 120 receiving nominations in categories such as Best Youth Development Program and Best Club/Organisation.   

The UAE Teen Sports & Fitness Awards, now in their fourth year, showcase the achievements of youths aged 11-19 across the country, with the winners being decided by a judging panel made up of local sport and fitness representatives.

This year’s results will be announced at a Gala dinner on Friday 26th April at Emirates Golf Club, Dubai.

For a full list of your 2019 finalists, click [here](https://www.teenawards.ae/2019-finalists.html).
