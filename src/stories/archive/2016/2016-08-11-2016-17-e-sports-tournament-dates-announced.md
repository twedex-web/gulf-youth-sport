After a hugely successful inaugural year, the E-Sports Silicon 7’s tournaments are back for season 2016-17.

Open to teams throughout the GCC, the tournaments offer competitive football with an emphasis on player development and fun.

**Dubai Stars Futsal Tournament**

**Date:** Friday 16 th September

**Venue:** Dubai Stars (Al Mizhar)

**Ages:** U6 – U14

**Format:** 5-a- side (8 per squad)

**Entry fee:** 700aed per team

**Silicon 7’s one-day Tournament**

**Date:** Friday 7 th October

**Venue:** Wellington Silicon Oasis

**Ages:** U6 – U16

**Format:** 7-a- side (10 per squad)

**Entry fee:** 700aed per team
