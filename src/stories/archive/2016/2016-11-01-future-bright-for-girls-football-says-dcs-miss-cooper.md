Dubai College U16 girls head coach, Miss Cooper, is excited about the future of girls football in the UAE.

The Scottish PE Teacher, 29, whose girls are undefeated in their first four matches of the DASSA season, spoke to the GYS cameras after an enthralling 2-2 draw with a strong WSO side.
