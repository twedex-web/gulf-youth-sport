Dates for the E-Sports Junior Football League (JFL) have been announced. The hugely popular leagues see the best in Dubai battle it out at GEMS Silicon Oasis on Thursday evenings.

**U6 – U12 JFL**

**Start date:** Thursday 27 TH October (runs for 6 weeks)

**Venue:** Wellington Silicon Oasis

**Ages:** U6 – U12

**Format:** 7-a- side (10 per squad)

**Entry fee:** 2500aed per team per season

**U14 – U16 JFL**

**Start date:** Thursday 20 TH October (runs for 6 weeks)

**Venue:** Wellington Silicon Oasis

**Ages:** U14 &amp; U16

**Format:** 7-a- side (10 per squad)

**Entry fee:** 2500aed per team per season

League places are extremely limited and coaches are encouraged to reserve their spots as soon as possible date to avoid disappointment.
