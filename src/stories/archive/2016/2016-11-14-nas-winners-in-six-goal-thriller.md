Nord Anglia International School under 10s came out on top in an emphatic 6-0 win over Dubai British School in the DAPSA on Thursday afternoon. Arjun, NAS’s star striker, scored a brace in the win and he gave his reaction after the match.

![](http://gys2.r-y-x.com/Admin/Content/UserFiles/14940041_651574341688401_7927901935930829960_o.jpg)

“\[I thought we were\] absolutely amazing! We were good with our spaces and we were good with our passing.”

Talking about his goal, Arjun said: “It was great play from the wing. Instead of taking a shot they crossed it in, and I managed to slide in and get it in the bottom corner.”
