**Saturday morning saw the culmination of a season of hard work in the Jebel Ali Youth League when Finals Day took place at the Centre of Excellence.**

Teams across five age groups have been battling it out for the right to be named JAYL Champions for the last nine weeks and the full list of winners of league titles and individual awards can be viewed below. 

A busy day of action saw a number of exciting games across six pitches with the presentation of medals and trophies following the completion of each round of fixtures.

A full image gallery from the presentation ceremonies will be available shortly. A huge well done to all the winners from everyone here at GYS!

For more information about the league please email info@itsjustfootball.com or visit the [website here](http://itsjustfootball.com/dubai-premier-youth-league).
