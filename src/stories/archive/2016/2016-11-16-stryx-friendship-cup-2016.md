**Highlights of the Stryx Friendship Cup, which took place at Jebel Ali Centre of Excellence on the 3rd of November, 2016.**

The competition, organised by the [Stryx Gulf Sports Services](http://stryxgulf.com/) team, was a one day event which saw hundreds of young footballers from all over Dubai come together to enjoy a day full of soccer and smiles.
