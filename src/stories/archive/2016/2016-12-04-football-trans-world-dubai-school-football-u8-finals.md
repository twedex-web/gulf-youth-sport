**The [Trans World](http://www.twedex.com/) Dubai School Football Finals kicked off today at Rashid School for Boys when the best of Dubai in the U8's category got to work.** 

Formarke School ran out winners of the A category, with a 1-0 victory over JBS in extra time. The whole afternoon was played in fantastic spirits with winning performances also from Hartland, Dubai International Academy, Horizon International School and Safa British School.
