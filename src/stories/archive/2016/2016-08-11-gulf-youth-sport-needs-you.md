Gulf Youth Sport is the most exciting thing to happen to youth sport in the Middle East for a generation, and we’re giving you the opportunity to be a part of it.

We’re looking for talented volunteers with a passion for sports and the media industry to join the GYS Media Team in the following areas:

-   Videographers
-   Photographers
-   Journalists
-   Presenters

As well as fantastic training and mentoring, GYS Media Team members also gain exclusive access to amazing offers and discounts with our partners and contributors.

To find out more, contact media@gulfyouthsoccer.com or sign up here: [http://gulfyouthsport.com/join-gys-team](/join-gys-team)
