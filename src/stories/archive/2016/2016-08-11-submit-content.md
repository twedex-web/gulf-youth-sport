If you have some exciting football news you’d like to share with the world, we want to hear about it!

You can either request coverage by emailing us at [info@gulfyouthsoccer.com](mailto:info@gulfyouthsoccer.com?subject=GYS%20Media%20Coverage%20Request), or you can send us your own content (news story/photos/videos)

If your article is finished and ready to go, we’ll check it then upload it straight away. If it’s not quite ready to go to print, or if it's really interesting and we want to find out all the juicy details, we’ll get in touch to find out more.
