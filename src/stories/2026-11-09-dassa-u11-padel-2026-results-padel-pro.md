The DASSA season is picking up speed. With fixtures now underway across Dubai, the under-11s took their turn on court on Thursday 5 November at the DASSA U11 Padel tournament, hosted at Padel Pro One Central.

Padel is one of the fastest-growing sports in UAE schools, and the turnout showed it, with young players from schools across the city lining up under the shadow of the Museum of the Future.

## Boys: Arcadia on Top

Arcadia British School claimed the boys' title, with Jumeirah Primary School (JPS) taking second place and JESS Jumeirah third.

## Girls: JPS Take Gold

JPS went one better in the girls' event, finishing first ahead of JESS Jumeirah in second and Hartland International School in third. That made JPS and JESS Jumeirah the only schools on the podium in both events.

## U11 Results

**Boys**

1. Arcadia British School
2. Jumeirah Primary School (JPS)
3. JESS Jumeirah

**Girls**

1. Jumeirah Primary School (JPS)
2. JESS Jumeirah
3. Hartland International School

Congratulations to every player who took part. With the DASSA calendar now in full swing, there is plenty more school sport to come this term.
