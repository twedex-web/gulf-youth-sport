# How to publish a story

For the Gulf Youth Sport team. You need a GitHub account that has been given access (ask the site admin).

1. Go to **gulfyouthsport.com/admin** and click **Login with GitHub**.
2. Click **+ Story**.
3. Copy each field from the article's Google Doc:
   - **Headline**: the headline.
   - **Slug**: the slug from the doc, e.g. `dassa-golf-june-2026`. Lower-case letters, numbers and hyphens only. This becomes the web address, so don't change it after publishing.
   - **Publish date**: already set to now. The newest date goes to the top of the homepage.
   - **Category**: the sport, e.g. Football, Swimming.
   - **Standfirst**: the one or two sentences under the headline.
   - **Featured image**: click **Choose an image**, then **Upload** and pick the Canva graphic.
   - **Featured image description**: a few words describing the graphic, e.g. "Players lifting the trophy". Leave blank if it's just text.
   - **Article**: paste the article text. Use the toolbar for subheadings, bold, links, quotes and lists.
   - **SEO title** and **Meta description**: from the doc. Leave blank to use the headline and standfirst.
4. Check the preview on the right, then click **Publish → Publish now**.

The site updates in about a minute. The new story is at the top of the homepage.

**To fix a mistake:** open the story from the list, edit it, and publish again.
**To remove a story:** open it and click **Delete entry**. Check with the editor first.

The Instagram brief stays in the Google Doc: it isn't published on the site.
