# How to publish a story

For the Gulf Youth Sport team. You need a GitHub account that has been given access (ask the site admin).

1. Go to **gulfyouthsport.com/admin** and click **Login with GitHub**.
2. Click **+ Story**.
3. Copy each field from the article's Google Doc:
   - **Headline**: the headline.
   - **Slug**: the slug from the doc, e.g. `dassa-golf-june-2026`. Lower-case letters, numbers and hyphens only. This becomes the web address, so don't change it after publishing.
   - **Publish date**: already set to now. The newest date goes to the top of the homepage.
   - **Category**: the sport, e.g. Football, Swimming.
   - **Standfirst**: the one or two sentences under the headline.
   - **Featured image**: click **Choose an image**, then **Upload** and pick the Canva graphic.
   - **Featured image description**: a few words describing the graphic, e.g. "Players lifting the trophy". Leave blank if it's just text.
   - **Article**: paste the article text. Use the toolbar for subheadings, bold, links, quotes and lists.
   - **SEO title** and **Meta description**: from the doc. Leave blank to use the headline and standfirst.
4. Check the preview on the right, then click **Publish → Publish now**.

The site updates in about a minute. The new story is at the top of the homepage.

**To fix a mistake:** open the story from the list, edit it, and publish again.
**To remove a story:** open it and click **Delete entry**. Check with the editor first.

The Instagram brief stays in the Google Doc: it isn't published on the site.

## Featured images

Upload the **photo only**, with no headline or text on it. The homepage shows the photo on the story's tile, with the headline underneath. If you only have the Instagram graphic, ask the site admin to crop it.

## Young people: what we publish

Most people in our stories are children. Before publishing, check:

- **Photos**: only publish photos where the school, club or event organiser has confirmed consent through its photo consent list.
- **Names**: for children under 13, use first names only unless the school has agreed full names can be used.
- **Never publish**: home addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, social media handles of children, or anything that says where a child can be found outside school or the event.
- **Ages**: an age group ("U13") is better than an exact age where possible.
- **Medical or personal details** (injuries, health, family circumstances) only with the family's agreement.

## Removal requests

People can ask us to correct or remove a story or photo: see gulfyouthsport.com/contact/#removal.

1. Forward every request to the editor straight away.
2. If it concerns a child, unpublish or edit the story the same day (open it in the admin, remove the photo or name, publish again), then look into the details.
3. Reply within 5 working days to confirm what was changed.
4. Keep the email, so there's a record that the request was handled.

For photos in the Zenfolio gallery, remove or hide them in Zenfolio.
